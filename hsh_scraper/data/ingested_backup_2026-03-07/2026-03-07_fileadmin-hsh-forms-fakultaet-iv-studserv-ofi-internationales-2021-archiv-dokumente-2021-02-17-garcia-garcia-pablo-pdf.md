Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
Very close to the Uni., Studierendenwohnheim RitterBrüning-Straße.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Café Glocksee, good parties and also you can be outside
with a beer with friends.


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
Yes I would. We went to many places, and we did many
actitivities. We went to Harz mountain, we played bubble
soccer.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Travel, visit the city, party.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Yeees! You learn a lot, good people, good university, it is
really cheap and one of the best cities.

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
Marketing. It was dynamic and interesting.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
That they are rude people sometimes, really serious.
They love beer.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
Yees! He was the best. His name is Meeno, and he
helped me a lot.

**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

The flexibility, the time tables, the lessons.

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Yes, it really has.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**
People, way of studying, culture

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**

**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes, I have.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yeees. Is a really nice city. I have some friends there, wanna visit them.
