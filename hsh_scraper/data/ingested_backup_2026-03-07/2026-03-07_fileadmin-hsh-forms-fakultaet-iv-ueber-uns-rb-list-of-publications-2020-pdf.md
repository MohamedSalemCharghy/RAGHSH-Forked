# Short CV


focused on Software Architectures, Intelligent Systems, bio-inspired Metaheuristics and their
applications. He received a Diploma degree and a Doctoral degree in Computer Science from
University of Oldenburg, Germany. During his studies, he was awarded a scholarship by the
Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes. Prof. Bruns is holding several research grants in the field
of innovative Software Architectures and Intelligent Systems, funded by the European
Community and the German Government. He has published more than 60 research papers and
co-authored two books on "Event-Driven Architecture: Softwarearchitektur für
ereignisgesteuerte Geschäftsprozesse" and "Complex Event Processing: Komplexe Analyse von
massiven Datenströmen mit CEP". He also serves as a member of the program committees for
international conferences and workshops.
