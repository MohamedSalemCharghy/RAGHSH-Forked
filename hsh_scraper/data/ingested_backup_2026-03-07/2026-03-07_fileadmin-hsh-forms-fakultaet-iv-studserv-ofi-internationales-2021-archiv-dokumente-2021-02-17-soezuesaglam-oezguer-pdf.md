Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
At the beginning, I have rented a house because I could
not gain a right to stay in Studentenwerk houses. After a
month, I have gained a place in one of the dormitories
(Am Papehof), then I have moved to the Am Papehof. I
have stayed until the end of semester.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
I think, Maschsee is my favorite. When I was in this
place, I was feeling more relaxed and I like the view that
combines the lake and nature.


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
I met with my Erasmus friends and I participated in
events that were organized by the university. I also tried
to discover the city.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I went running because there are lots of beautiful paths
near to my dormitory. I was also chilling with my friends
from my university and dormitory.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
If they want to live in a quiet and calm city, I mostly
recommend Hannover because there is no crowd of
people. I also recommend Hannover because there are
lots of natural areas that you can make some activities
like trekking.


**Which transport did you use to get to the university,**
**and to travel around the city?**
I mostly used the subway because it worked perfectly,
and I had an opportunity to reach almost everywhere. I
also used local trains for specific destinations.


**Where did you have lunch?**
I mainly used the university canteen, because it is
cheaper and delicious. Other times I tried restaurants at
Kröpcke.

Incoming-Interview


**What were you surprised about, in regard to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Actually I am familiar with German culture and lifestyle, because my father was born in Germany. They
are always punctual and want to do something on-time. I was relatively surprised that German people
don’t stop doing sport, even if the air is freezing cold.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around the university, and did the same person**
**help you also in daily life, or someone different?**
The university had assigned a buddy to us. The name of my buddy was Kahraman. He helped me a lot.
There were also WhatsApp groups that students could help each other and organize some activities.


**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**

All professors I had were very kind and helpful. They always wanted to help, when we were confronted
with problems. They were good-humored to me, and I felt very relaxed when communicating with them.

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
Of course, I have started to think master programs, instead of starting directly to work.


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
Presentation skills; increased both German and English skills; and unique recommendations about my
study.

**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home** ?
Of course, I am communicating with both university friends and dormitory friends.

**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Of course, I am planning to come back Hannover either for touristic purposes, or for working because I
am curious about the feeling that will occur when I come back to Hannover. I have also lots of people
from Hannover and without doubt I will miss them so much.
