Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
I found a room in a Student Wohnheim located in
Herrenhausen Markt. I stayed there for 2 semesters, and
I was sharing a kitchen with other 7 roommates from
other countries.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
I reckon I have so many favourite places in Hannover, but
if I had to choose, I would go for Limmerstrasse and its
great and young atmosphere. I’ve been there so many
times in order to take away diverse kinds of food, such as
Vietnamese cuisine.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Fortunately, I could afford to buy a second-hand bicycle,
so I could ride along the city. Furthermore, I found places
I never thought I would see, like Rickingen Bad. So I
spent plenty of my time looking for unknown places in
Hannover. I think that’s why I love this city so much.

Anyway, I registered for Campusliga and I played football
during my Erasmus. I played with other Erasmus, so I
had a great time in many afternoons. As an Erasmus
Student, I also went on many nights out, and enjoyed
planned parties by ESN, and international parties in
Student Wohnheims.

**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
One of the things that got me surprised was the empathy
and kindness of most of people. I was amazed by the
cordiality and great treatment of foreign people.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
I didn’t have any concrete buddy here in Hannover.
However, there were international people who helped me
very much during the first weeks.

**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover? For example; the professors, the courses,**
**the equipment etc..**
I generally liked the teachers, and the equipment we
received to work on projects, such as recording rooms,
nice libraries, computer rooms… and many more

Incoming-Interview


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
Of course. In fact, I’ve been looking for an internship in Germany, especially in Hannover. I would like to
stay in Germany for the following years, by taking a Masters or doing internships there.

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
Hannover is and will be my second home. Forever. I’ve always felt cozy and comfortable, in a green and
sustainable city in Germany. Is not as big as Madrid, and that’s why I feel so good.

**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes. We created an excellent group, in which we contact every week. We have met many times after
Erasmus and our relationship has enhanced.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
I hope in a near future I am there doing an internship or a Masters. I am in-love with Hannover, and I
would never forget my Erasmus experience there.
