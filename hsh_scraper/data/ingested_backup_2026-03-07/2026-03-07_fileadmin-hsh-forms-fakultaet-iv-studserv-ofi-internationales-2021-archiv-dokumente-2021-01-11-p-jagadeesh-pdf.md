**Which was your favorite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
My favorite place is Messe grounds and Hannover zoo.

**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Sure, I would recommend, and I have already referred
my junior she is coming this September, because it not
just the place to learn education, it is the place where
good self-discipline of the people, policy of government,
Infrastructure, freedom to do interesting profession and
good work life balance. Laws are placed in once place,
so people who are traveling can learn and realise a lot .

**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Discipline, respect for others, time keep-up, dedication
and enjoy work life balance.

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Sure, I am applying for the internship here.

**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Self-Discipline

- Be creative

- Enjoying work life balance

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
It’s unbeatable, chances and best times of the life.

**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
I made almost 25 friends from over 8 countries, (In
coming years surely I will travel to their homes).

**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

I like the discipline that everybody follows there, that in
turn gave me lot of teachings. Also almost all problems
are sorted here (water, sanitation, transport), so mostly
people think same problems in a much deeper and
rooted level. That induced me to think creative at certain
areas, to reflect back onto my career .
