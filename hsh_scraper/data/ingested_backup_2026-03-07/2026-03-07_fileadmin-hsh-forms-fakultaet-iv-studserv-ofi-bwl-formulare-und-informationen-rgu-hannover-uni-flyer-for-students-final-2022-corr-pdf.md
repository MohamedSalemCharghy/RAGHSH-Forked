**INTRODUCING ABERDEEN CITY AND ROBERT**
**GORDON UNIVERSITY**


Aberdeen is Scotland’s third largest city, celebrated
for its proximity to the sea and its famous sparkling
architecture. Living and studying in Aberdeen means
you will be part of an energetic city with 25,000
students from across the globe. You can enjoy
unrivalled food and drink experiences, year-round
festivals and events, art galleries, museums, a golden
beach, parks and gardens and even picture-perfect old
towns.


Thanks to its position in Scotland the opportunity for
outdoor adventure is vast too. Try out paddle boarding
through sea caves, surfing city centre waves, hiking
in the Cairngorms National Park, hitting the slopes at
Snowsports Centres or even conquering challenging
cycling terrains.


Robert Gordon University is an exciting blend of the
traditional and the contemporary. Founded in 1729
the University has a long history in education, yet in
2013 it became the UK’s most modern campus with
the opening of its £170 Million campus development in
Aberdeen.


RGU is famed for being an innovative, accessible,
impactful and professionally minded university. RGU’s
applied learning, industry-centric approach, modern
facilities and contemporary course content, enable
students to prepare for successful careers in a variety
of professional fields.



**ABOUT THE COURSE**


The double degree starts only once a year in the winter
term/ September, beginning at HsH, with the following
term starting at RGU in January. The 3rd term also at
RGU covers mainly the thesis, the hand in of the thesis
is usually in August.


Hochschule Hannover Master of Science Business
Development MBD. The details of the course and how
to apply can be found at

**https://f4.hs-hannover.de/studium/master-studienga**
**enge/unternehmensentwicklung-mbd**


Robert Gordon University MSc International Business.
The details of the course can be found at : www.rgu.
ac.uk/internationalbusiness and details of how to apply
here: **www.rgu.ac.uk/apply**


When applying select September start date for the
course, and write in the notes that you are through this
partnership to begin in January with RGU.


**COURSE STRUCTURE**


**Grey – Hochschule Hannover**


**Purple – Course Robert Gordon University**


**Semester 1**


Complete 5 modules for 30 ECTS credits:


- MBD-611 Internationales
Wertschöpfungsmanagement


- MBD-612 Change Management


- MBD-614 Weltwirtschaftliche Entwicklung


- MBD-615 Forschungsmethoden


- MBD-624 Internationales Marketing


**Semester 2**


Complete 4 modules for 30 ECTS credits:


- BSM017 Finance for Managers

- BSM018 Economics of Business

- BSM840 International Business Strategy

- BSM538 Strategic Leadership


**Semester 3**


Capstone or placement project = 30 ECTS credits

**FEES**


The fees for students coming from the EU directly
into semester 2 at RGU in January 2023 will be £4,635
including the 10% discount for HsH students.


Please note fees listed are for EU students permenatly
resident in the EU. For any international students an
international fee may be payable.


**DEGREE AWARD:**


Upon completion of this double degree, students will
receive a Masters in Business Planning from Hochschule
Hannover and a Masters of Science in International
Business from Robert Gordon University.
