Incoming-Interview


**Do you think that you will come back**
**to Hannover in the future? – If so,**
**why / If not, why not?**
I think I would come back to Hannover,
maybe in my future career. Overall, I like
the city, and I would see myself maybe
working there or opening a business

**What lasting impression will you have**

.

**of Hannover?**
Great experience. Awesome place to be
studying in.



**Which was your favorite place in Hannover and why?**
The Erlebnis Zoo was most definitely my favorite place.
Initially I was thinking of a bar or the big park, but my top
one is the zoo.

**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
During the introduction week, I got to meet the professors
and students. I received my documents, different useful
stuff, and later I got my student card. I also learned more
about the university itself, like where stuff is located, and
how to use the library and printing machines and such.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I was involved in a lot of activities with my host family in
Hameln. I went to dirt bike racing and hiking. I also did
visit other cities like Bremen, Berlin, Munich and
Frankfurt.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Yes, I would recommend it to other people because it’s a
great experience overall. The teachers and students are
very kind and helpful, and there’s a ton of things to do.


**What was your favorite subject and why?**
My favorite subject was Business Administration, taught
by Mr. Austmann. We didn’t just learn about ethics the
regular way, but also from a new, modern worlds
perspective. We learned how to manage a business,
while at the same time taking care of the environment,
and the future generations. I remember we’d stay after
class and talk more about this subject.

**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

Great professors. Enthusiastic and kind. Courses were
interesting and helpful. I came from a software
engineering background, so a lot of definitions and
subjects were strange to me, but I quickly caught on.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Most definitely it got me thinking more of the impact on
the environment, Mr. Austmann’s courses.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Great food

- Being on time like Germans

- Save the planet!
