#  Information for Incomings 
## Welcome to Faculty I – Electrical Engineering and Information Technology
Thank you for your interest in our university, we would be happy to welcome you at our faculty.
Every year, students from different countries study at University of Applied Sciences and Arts in Hannover. The main language in our bachelor and master degree programmes is German. However, we offer some courses in English language every winter term for our exchange students (see under study options).
Faculty I is one of five faculties. The Adress of our campus in Linden: Ricklinger Stadtweg 120, 30459 Hannover.

## Study offer
#### Offer of English courses
The general language of study is German.
However, **every winter term** the faculty additionally offers a selection of technical and non-technical subjects which are taught in English language. This offer is especially open for our international students from our partner universities. For an overview of the English lectures in the current winter term please **see under study options.**
#### Study Programs in German
Generally, a bachelor program takes 7 semesters to complete, including a practical placement. The degree programs taught in German are accredited and internationally recognized. Study options in the bachelor degree program include standard and dual-track programs for Electrical Engineering and Information Technology.
#### Bachelor Degrees
  * B.Eng. Industrial Electrical Engineering (EWI)
  * B.Eng. Mechatronics (MEC)
  * B.Eng. Technical Information Design and Technical Communication (ITR)
  * B.Eng. Electrical Engineering and Information Technology (EIT) 
  * B.Sc. Applied Mathematics (MAT)


#### Master Degrees (3 semesters)
  * M.Eng. Sensor and Automation Technology (ESA)
  * M.Eng. Electrical Power Systems and E-Mobility (EEE)
