## What does I-PraC stand for?
The programme „International Practice Cooperation for Global Change (I-PraC) is designed to provide students with international practical experience through close cooperation with partners abroad.
With I-PraC, we want to assume responsibility as global citizens and develop the required labour market skills for the future. The project anchors its activities on the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) and incorporates the concept of Education for Sustainable Development (ESD) of UNESCO. The implementation takes place in the curricula and the transfer at a local and a global level.
Interdisciplinary work will be tested, since I-PraC was initially being conceived in the Faculty III (Media, Information and Design) , Faculty IV (Business and Computer Science) and Faculty II (Mechanical and Bioprocess Engineering ). Further faculties are welcome to join!
Last but not least, the project aims to be part of the development of the internationalisation and digitalisation at the HsH in a broad and structural way. It should serve as a pilot to implement internationalisation at all university levels and across all groups - students, teaching staff, researchers and administrative staff.

**I-PraC: Call for grants for stays abroad in 2023**
Are you a HsH-student and do you want to complete your internship abroad? **Are you keen to work for the SDGs and their related areas?** We anticipate that you are interested in international cooperation and global engagement and invite you to apply for an I-PraC scholarship.

  * Willingness to particicipate in surveys before, during and after the mobility

## Teaching and Learning Events
[ Invitation: Teaching and Learning Sustainability 2023 ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/weltweit-vernetzt/internationale-projekte/international-practice-cooperation-for-global-change-i-prac "Invitation: Teaching and Learning Sustainability 2023")
**Teaching and Learning Sustainability at HsH**
The topic of sustainable development is becoming increasingly important in all areas of life. Universities have an important role to play here, because they train professionals, who will make decisions in the future.
Teaching and learning should therefore be oriented in such a way that it also enable students to act as agents in the sense of sustainable development and positive transformation in their careers and society.
Thus, this workshop is designed as an exchange on following aspects:
  * Where does the topic of sustainability already have space in teaching?
  * How are sustainability issues dealt with?
  * How can higher education promote sustainability competences?


**Target group:** Teachers from all faculties at HsH

_**Nachhaltigkeit lehren und lernen an der HsH**_
_Das Thema nachhaltige Entwicklung gewinnt in allen Lebensbereichen immer mehr an Bedeutung. Hochschulen kommt dabei eine wichtige Rolle zu, denn sie bilden Fachkräfte aus, die zukünftig Entscheidungen treffen werden._
_Lehre und Lernen sollten deshalb so ausgerichtet sein, dass sie Studierende (auch) dazu befähigen, als aktive Gestalter*innen im Sinne einer nachhaltigen Entwicklung bzw. Transformation in Beruf und Gesellschaft zu handeln._
_In diesem Workshop steht der Austausch über diese Anforderung an Lehre im Mittelpunkt:_
  * _Wo hat das Thema Nachhaltigkeit schon Raum in der Lehre?_
  * _Wie wird mit Nachhaltigkeitsthemen gearbeitet?_
  * _Wie kann Hochschulbildung Nachhaltigkeitskompetenzen fördern?_
