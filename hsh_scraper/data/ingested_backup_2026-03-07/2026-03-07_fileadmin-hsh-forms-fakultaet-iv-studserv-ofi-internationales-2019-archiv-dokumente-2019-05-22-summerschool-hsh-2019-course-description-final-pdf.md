# **Syllabus Digital Transformation – Challenges and Impacts on Economy and Society**











|Persons in Charge|Prof. Dr. Lars Baumann, Prof. Dr. Andreas Hausotter, Prof. Dr. Dagmar Mack, Dr.<br>Dagmar Ridder (GISMA), Prof. Dr. Maylin Wartenberg|
|---|---|
|**Language of Instruction **|<br>English|
|**Course Type**|Lectures with exercises|
|**ECTS Credits**|6|
|**Contact Hours**|60 hrs|
|**Duration of Course**|2 weeks, 10-Aug-2019 to 24-Aug-2019|
|**Recommended**<br>**Prerequisites **|First phase of study program|
|<br>**Suggestions for**<br>**Independent Study **|• Preparation and follow-up based on lectures<br>• Organization in groups for work on case studies in workshops<br>• Preparation for the exam|
|**Examination**|Group Presentation (= results of workshops), 20 min|
|**Learning Outcomes **|Students understand the different aspects of digitalization, its impacts on economy,<br>society and know different methods of digital transformation.They know the different<br>dimensions of digitalization, such as transforming business models, process<br>organizations, organizational structures, IT. Students know main IT Architectures for<br>digitalization. They also know main enablers of digitalization like Big Data, Data Mining,<br>Artificial Intelligence, collaboration. Theyare able to apply learned methods of digital<br>transformation, e.g. design thinking, working out loud, group model building, build-<br>measure-learn-loop. Managing big data and analytics are the key enablers of<br>digitalization. Therefore, students also learn different Business Intelligence applications<br>and Data Mining methods. They are able to apply these methods to real problems by<br>using appropriate tools. Group exercises and workshops during several case studies<br>support the enhancement of social competencies.<br>|
|**Content**|**1. Digitalization & Digital Transformation**<br>• Introduction / Dimensions / Enablers / Methods<br>• Opportunities / Risks / Challenges<br>• Impacts on Economy and Society<br>• Focus: Transforming Sales Processes<br>• Case Studies / Exercises<br>**2. IT Architectures for Digitalization**<br>• Overview / Methods<br>• Focus: Domain Driven Design<br>• Case Studies / Exercises<br>**3. Business Intelligence & Data Mining**<br>• Overview BI Applications / Reporting / Dashboards / OLAP<br>• Introduction to Data Mining<br>• Data Mining Process: Business Understanding / Data Understanding (Attributes –<br>Visualization – Correlation, …) / Data Preparation (Selecting Data – Transformation –<br>Feature Selection) / Modeling (Patterns – Decision & Regression Trees – Neural<br>Networks, ...) / Deployment<br>• Case Studies / Exercises<br>**4. Workshops (Parallel to 1. – 3.)**<br>**5. Excursions / Socializing & Intercultural Activities (Visiting Berlin / Hanover / …)**|
