#  Information | exchange students 
Thank you for your interest in our university. We would be happy to welcome you at our faculty!  
Faculty IV - Business and Computer Science - is one of five faculties at the University of Applied Sciences and Arts. The Faculty IV is divided into three departments:  
Business Administration, Business Information Systems and Computer Science.
The faculty provides scientifically sound courses on a high level which qualify for careers and also include diverse opportunities for students to enhance their international and intercultural competencies. See for yourself and study with us!
The Team International Programmes (TIP) of Faculty IV, would be happy to counsel and support you and to answer all your questions around courses, credits, requirements, and much else.  
Please feel free to contact us!
**Email:**[f4-tip@hs-hannover.de](https://f4.hs-hannover.de/internationales/information-for-exchange-students)
