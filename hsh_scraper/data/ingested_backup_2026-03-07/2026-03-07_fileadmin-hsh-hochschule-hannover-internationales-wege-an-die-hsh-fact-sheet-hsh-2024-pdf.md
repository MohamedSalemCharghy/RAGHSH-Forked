**Does the university charge tuition fees?** In Germany, tuition fees (“Studiengebühren”) are
mostly abolished. However, a semester fee
(“Semesterbeitrag”) has to be paid by students.
This is approximately 400.00 € per semester.
Exchange students are exempt from the included
administrative fee and therefore pay a reduced
amount (around 325.00 €).
