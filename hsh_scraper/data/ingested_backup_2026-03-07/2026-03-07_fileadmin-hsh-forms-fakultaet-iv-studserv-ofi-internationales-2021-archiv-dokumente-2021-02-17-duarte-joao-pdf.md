Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
Student residence, Am Papehof 10.

**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
The Maschsee and the Ricklinger lakes because of the
overwhelming contact with nature. Also, some skateparks
like the 2ER and the Welfen Platz, because I made
friends there and always spent good time there.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
I would recommend because there was no big problem
when I was there, transports are good, calm city, low
crime rate.

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
All classes with Henning, sustainable development.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

The classrooms were well equipped, the professors were
very respectful, and the teaching methods were also very
good, with a weekly number of hours well distributed.

**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Timing

- Organization

- Sustainability

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
Very calm and peaceful place.

**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, always a good place to visit, but maybe not on the
top of the list because I already lived there. I want to see
other places in the world now ☺

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Sustainably wise and importance of organization.
