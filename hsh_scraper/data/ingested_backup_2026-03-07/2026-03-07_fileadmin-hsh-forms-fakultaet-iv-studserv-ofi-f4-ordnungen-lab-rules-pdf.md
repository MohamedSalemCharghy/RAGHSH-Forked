# **Lab rules (department of business)**

Conduct in the laboratories and at all workstations


- Smoking, eating, and drinking at workstations and in the laboratories is not allowed

- Please do not use cell phones while in the laboratories

- Do not leave paper waste behind

- Workstations should be left in a proper condition (e . g . bring back chairs)

- Lectures always have first priority in using the workstations

- The laboratories are not to be used as waiting or break rooms

- Paper and toner are not free. lf you supply your own paper or transparencies, you will still
be charged full price for printing. At the beginning of the semester every student receives
a certain amount of free credit on their printing account

- Other materials will not be supplied by the department
