Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
In the closest dormitory at Papehof.

**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Since I’m a huge football fan I really loved the HDI arena,
and went to the matches every second weekend

.
**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
I attended the German course to improve my German
and besides I made some friends, so I would recommend
it without any hesitation.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I got to know some Germans, who helped me in literally
everything, and we hung out a lot together. I even joined
a local football club, which I loved more than anything
else.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
The professors are amazing, and students are nice, so
the uni was fun. I was hardly ever bored, we had always
something to do. All in all, I feel that I learnt a lot, what I
would have missed, if I had not come.

**What was your favorite subject and why?**
I did not have a favorite one, but I had a favourite
professor Mr. Henning Austmann, who was more than a
professor. He was more a life coach, who provided us life
lessons, which can be helpful in the rest of our life. He
always tried to get me out of my comfort zone and push
me to my limits. I’m really thankful to him, for everything
what he did for me.

**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Germans proved to be strict on rules, hardworking,
precious and organized. On the other hand, they can be
cool and funny just as well, and they are really nice when
it comes to the welcome.

**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
I had a good friend of mine in the city, before I would
have come, so he helped me on my first days.
Afterwards, I got a buddy on my side, who helped me to
integrate into the new society.

Incoming-Interview


**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**
I loved the way of teaching. Classes were interactive all the time and professors forced us to be active.
The professors had excellent knowledge, and they were always ready to answer all my questions.

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**

Definitely yes. We learnt a lot about sustainability, which made me think what my priorities should be. I
will not chase the economic growth at any price, because it could/will backfire.


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
Being open minded is key to success.
Team work is way more effective, than working by myself.
There is always some place to improve.

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
The loving big city, which is more a town than a city, though there are half a million people.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Some promised me to come to visit my place, so hopefully we will stay in touch.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
I made a lot of friends in Hannover, so I would not close out a return, but I do not see my future clearly.
