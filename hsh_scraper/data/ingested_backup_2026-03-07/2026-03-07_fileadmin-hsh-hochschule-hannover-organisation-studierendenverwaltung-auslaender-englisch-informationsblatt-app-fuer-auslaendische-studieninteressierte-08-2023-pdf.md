# **Information for prospective foreign students and for**
## **prospective German students with educational certificates** **from countries other than Germany** **General Information**

The Hannover University of Sciences and Arts (HsH – Hochschule Hannover) introduced an
external examination procedure to take place before the actual application procedure for
prospective students from foreign countries and for prospective German students with
educational certificates from countries other than Germany.

This formal examination has been carried out by “UNI-ASSIST”, the work and service center
jointly operated by universities, to process applications from international students for a fee,
[see link https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/.](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/)

**Please be sure to note that an application made to the HsH will not be processed by**
**uni-assist unless it includes proof of the applicant’s language proficiency at a level of**
**at least B1 for German (Goethe Zertifikat B 1,1, telc B 1 or ÖSD B 1). Language**
**certificates from private language institutions will not be accepted by the HsH.**


Once you have this language certificate, please register and submit your application online to
[uni-assist at www.uni-assist.de.](http://www.uni-assist.de/) [Apply in six steps: https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/)
[apply/.](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/)


The Hannover University of Sciences and Arts (HsH – Hochschule Hannover) accepts
[online-only applications via uni assist using non-certified copies of certificates.](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/details/online-only-application/)
An online application with uploaded documents is sufficient.


Uni-assist will check the educational certificates you have from your home country to
determine whether you can be directly admitted to university or whether you will first need to
go to the Studienkolleg, a preparation course. If the formal examination of your documents is
positive, UNI-ASSIST will forward your application on your behalf to the Hochschule
Hannover or another university, if applicable. We (Hochschule Hannover) will then decide
whether or not you will be accepted for admission.

If the formal examination of your documents by uni-assist is negative (no direct admission),
you can contact the Hochschule Hannover directly, enclosing the letter from UNI-ASSIST
and an explanation for any errors in the evaluation made by UNI-ASSIST. We will then check
your application again and decide whether or not it will be possible for you to study at our
university.

Go to [https://www.daad.de/en/ to find the official admission requirements for university](https://www.daad.de/en/)
admission from your home country.


## **Documents**

**Your application to study at the Hochschule Hannover must include the following**
**documents, please only upload the documents**

- Curriculum vitae (in tabular form)

- Complete passport, with residence permit, if applicable
(personal ID card for German and EU applicants will suffice)

- School records (school-leaving certificate, A- and O-levels, high school diploma,
baccalaureat, gaocao, etc.)

- University admission exam from your home country, if applicable

- University records, if you have begun or completed studies in Germany or your
home country

- Assessment test certificate, if applicable (completion of a Studienkolleg in Germany)

- Translations of all the educational certificates listed in the above
**(only English documents need not be translated)**

- Applicants from China, Vietnam and **since 01.11.2022 from India** must also
upload APS certificate

- Proof of language proficiency in German (the B1 certificate is sufficient for your application).
Proof of language proficiency at the higher level (among others, TestDaF 4 x 4 see below
under Language Certificates for direct university admission) must be produced until
15 January for summer semester and 15 July for winter semester. Otherwise, the
application will not be possible to take part at the selection process for the respective
degree program.

## **Language requirements**

**Language skills required for direct admission to the university**


- German language examination for university admission (registered DSH-2 certificate)

- Großes or Kleines Sprachdiplom from the Goethe Institut = C 2

- Zentrale Oberstufenprüfung (ZOP) from the Goethe Institut = C 2

- Goethe-Zertifikat = C 2

- Test Deutsch als Fremdsprache (TestDaF) Niveaustufe 4 mit 4 x 4 Punkten (TDN4)

- telc Sprachzertifikat C 1 Niveau – Deutsch Hochschule

- Deutsches Sprachdiplom from the Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK) – Zweite Stufe –

- Feststellungsprüfungszeugnis (Language skills assessment certificate)

- At least a secondary school leaving certificate from a German school in Germany

- At least a secondary school leaving certificate from a German school abroad
(where German university entrance qualifications are awarded)

- Proof of German language skills recognized by bilateral agreements or other
agreements concluded by the KMK and HRK (German Rectors' Conference) as
sufficient proof of language proficiency for admission to higher education.


## **Important note:**

Hannover University of Applied Sciences and Arts does not offer language
preparation courses and language examinations for “external” applicants.

**Language skills required for the preparatory college**

Recognized German certificates of at least level B 1 can be submitted

i.a. Onset,
TestDaF, DSH, DSD I and II, telc, telc Hochschule, Goethe certificates, ÖSD certificates,
Unicert, IB- German, certificates from the TU Clausthal, certificates from the TU
Braunschweig. Certificates from other test providers will not be accepted.

**The following application procedure applies here:**


The Lower Saxony **Studienkolleg** at Leibniz University Hannover prepares foreign
applicants whose educational certificates do not allow direct access to higher education in
preparatory courses for study at a university.

If you have to attend Studienkolleg in advance, please not the following application process:

After checking your application documents via uni-assist, you will receive information about
the notification of the admission procedure for the Studienkolleg from the student
administration at Hochschule Hannover.

You will receive all further information directly from Studienkolleg.

Important information about the admission procedure:

The Studienkolleg will ask you to send your current language certificate, which will be used
for the selection for the entrance test.

Based on the certificates Studienkollg will make a preselection and send out the invitations to
the entrance test. If you have very good language skills (more than B 1), you have the best
chance of receiving an invitation.

Please note that invitations to the entrance test are only sent by Studienkolleg. The
Hochschule Hannover has no influence on sending of the invitation to the entrance test.

You can find more information about this procedure on the homepage Studienkolleg
[https://www.stk.uni-hannover.de/en/course-participation/entrance-test](https://www.stk.uni-hannover.de/en/course-participation/entrance-test)

**If you don’t receive an invitation to the entrance test or if you don’t pass the entrance**
**test, you are welcome to contact us again. Then we can consider your application**
**again next semester. Please include your applicant number. Otherwise we cannot help**
**you and you will have to apply again via uni-assist.**

Once your application has been successfully checked by uni-assist and you can prove that
you have direct university admission, your application will be forwarded to the Hochschule

**your application and recall your admission later.**

## **Applications to several different universities**


The formal check made by UNI-ASSIST gives you the opportunity to apply to several
universities that also require your documents to be checked by UNI-ASSIST with only one
set of application documents (this currently applies to approx. 170 of the 348 universities in
Germany). But, PAY ATTENTION, depending on the university or the course of studies, you


might need to submit different documents! You can find all “UNI-ASSIST universities” at
[https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/uni-assist-universities/.](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/uni-assist-universities/)

**Please note:** Applications to universities that do not use the UNI-ASSIST process will not be
processed by UNI-ASSIST, nor will they be forwarded to the university they are intended for.

## **Fees**

According to the resolution of the General Assembly of 28.03.2014 of UNI-ASSIST, the fee
charged for the formal examination carried out by UNI-ASSIST amounts to a one-time
payment of **€ 75** for your first application as from 1 October 2014.

From 1 March 2018, a processing fee of € 30 must be paid for each extra application made
within one semester in addition to the first application. This applies regardless of whether
applications for several subjects are submitted to one university or to different universities.

Your application will not be processed by UNI-ASSIST until the fee has been paid.
[For more detailed information, go to: https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/)
