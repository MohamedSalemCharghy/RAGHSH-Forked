#  Application Process 
### **Application Process for students from partner universities - How to apply**
  1. Please check which **English-taught courses** will be offered in winter term (see under Study Options).
  2. Students from Partner universities: Your home university will send a **nomination** to Hochschule Hannover. The deadline for nominations for winter termin is 1 May.
  3. **Afterwards the HsH-Incoming Team / Advisory Service will send you an email with all the information on how to apply.** You can contact them via email to: hsh-incoming@hs-hannover.de. The deadline for applications for winter term is 15 May.
  4. After application, please send your **Learning Agreement** with your course list to: [viola.wedekind(at)hs-hannover.de](https://f1.hs-hannover.de/studium/internationales/information-for-incomings/application-process) .
