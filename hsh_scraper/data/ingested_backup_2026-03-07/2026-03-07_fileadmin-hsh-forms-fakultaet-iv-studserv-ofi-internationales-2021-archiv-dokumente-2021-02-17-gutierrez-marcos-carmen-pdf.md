Incoming-Interview



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Tier Garten because I love animals


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Being with my friends, meet new people, visit new
places...


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and why?**
Absolutely, it has really good technology and nice
professors.


**What did you like about studying at Hannover**
**university? The professors? The courses? The**
**equipment?**
Everything, specially the chance to choose English
courses


**What 3 key things will you take away with you from**
**your studies in Hannover?**
German language, new experiences and new friends


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
I realized it is one of experiences of my life


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so why/If not why not?**
I hope, I really want to come back, because it’s a nice
place and I have a lot of memories there


**What were you surprised about, in regard to**
**stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
They are punctual.


**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
Bischofsholer Damm
