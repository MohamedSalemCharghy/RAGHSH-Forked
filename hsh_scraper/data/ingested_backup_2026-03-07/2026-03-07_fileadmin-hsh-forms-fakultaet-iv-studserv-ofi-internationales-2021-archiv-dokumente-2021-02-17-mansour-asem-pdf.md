Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
At a shared apartment in Misburg.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why** ?
Altwarmbüchener See. The view of the lake and the open
spaces of the forest are the best things about that place.
The lake is also near a good shopping mall (A2 center).


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
We basically had a couple of tours around the campus
and Hannover. Also, an introductory meeting was made
for all incomings. I would definitely recommend it.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I mainly travelled around countries neighboring Germany,
(the Netherlands, the Czech Republic, Denmark,
Sweden). Locally, I travelled to the majority of cities
covered by the Semester-Ticket, + some cities that were
not covered (Berlin, Frankfurt, Dortmund). In Hannover, I
used to spend time sightseeing monuments/places and
going to local fairs like Oktoberfest and Weihnachtsmarkt.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
I would recommend HSH to other students because the
education system there delivered the technical and
practical approaches promised. The perks of Hannover
being an underrated city in Germany adds to the
experience of HSH as well.

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
Entrepreneurship in a Global Context. IBS-250-1. I really
liked this subject because it dealt directly with the “grind
and hustle” of the entrepreneurial environment. I still use
some of what I have learned from that class in some of
my other class in my home university.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
The stagnant stereotype that “Germans are always
serious and not fun” was almost nonexistent whenever I
had to deal with German nationals. Germans also spoke
good English in more contexts and workplaces than I
expected.

Incoming-Interview


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around the university, and did the same person**
**help you also in daily life, or someone different?**
To be honest, my apartment host, who is also my friend, helped me a lot in the first days. Germany was
not my first experience abroad, so I adapted quickly in 3-4 days. After that I was on my own “autopilot”
for the rest of the semester.


**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**
It is a blend of 3. The professors were very knowledgeable, spoke fluent English and were great at
enriching the classes. The courses were mostly tailored to be optimal for our learning process. The labs,
library and studying halls were readily available most of the time.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
It had made me consider Germany for further academics/jobs in the future.


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
Erasmus memories with great friends. Travelling to multiple European countries, and the quality of the
education.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
A quiet, but an underrated German city, optimal for academics and travelling.

**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, if I have the chance to do so I will, because I want to revisit some of the memorable people/places
there.
