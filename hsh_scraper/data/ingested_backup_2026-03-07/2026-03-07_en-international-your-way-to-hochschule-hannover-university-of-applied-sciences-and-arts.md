### [Why Hannover?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ufWkH13W2ig)
The greenest major city in Germany has a lot to offer. The capital of Lower Saxony is quite centrally located in Germany. One of the largest public festivals in northern Germany is celebrated here every summer. 530 kilometres of cycle paths make it easy to get around the city. More good reasons to visit Hannover can be found on the city's website:www.visit-hannover.com. You find good reasons for studying at our university here.
