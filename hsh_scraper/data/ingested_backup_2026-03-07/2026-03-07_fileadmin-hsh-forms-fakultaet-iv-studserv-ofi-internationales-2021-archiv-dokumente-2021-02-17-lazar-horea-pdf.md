Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
Jägerstraße: Morwegsgasse and Lodyweg, 30167
Hannover.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Nordstadt, because of the pretty architecture and cool
Kiosks.

**What did you do in your spare/free time** ?
Walked around the city a lot, exploring it. I also visited a
lot of surrounding cities, some even in other countries,
during my weekends. I had been to Berlin 3 times,
Hamburg and Bremen twice, Köln, Hildesheim a lot
(having a friend who was doing Erasmus there).
Brunswick, Osnabruck, Celle, Gothenburg, Leipzig and
Dresden. As mentioned, I travelled for a weekend to the
Baltic countries, on another occasion I had been to
Groningen.

**After your semester at HannoverUniversity, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Yes, as a matter of fact I think one student contacted me
in 2019 and I suggested that he go to Hannover. I found
the city to be quite diverse and fun. The university life
was also a top experience, in the sense that the courses
seemed very well organized, and the projects were
diverse and plentiful.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Well in my experiences, studying in the German school
back in Romania, I was told a lot about German culture,
lifestyle and stereotypes. Staying longer in Hanover, I
have indeed found that Germans are hard-working and
really play by the rules, without exceptions. I have found
them to be a tad more distant in social situations.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

I liked the professors, they were kind enough to explain in
English too, and would sit even after courses to clarify
any misunderstandings.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, for sure! I would like to revisit the city and remember
all the great things that happened there. Walk the same
streets again, while listening to the same albums, and
see what has changed in the meantime.
