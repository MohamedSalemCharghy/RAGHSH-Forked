## Before your stay
[ Visa/ Entry Requirements ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Visa/ Entry Requirements")
EU citizens do not need a visa or a residence permit to enter the Federal Republic of Germany. International students from countries outside the EU usually require a visa. Therefore please do check the information concerning visa shared by the Federal Foreign Office:
[Visa requirements](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/-/231148)
[Visa regulations](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service/-/215870)
The type of visa needed may vary due to purpose and/or length of your stay in Germany. Therefore please do make sure to inform yourself carefully what type of visa is needed for your stay. The Federal Foreign Office offers an online tool to support in visa application:
[Visa navigator](https://visa.diplo.de/en/index.html#/vib)
More information concerning visa can also be found here:
[Study in Germany](https://www.study-in-germany.de/en/plan-your-studies/requirements/visa/)
[ Proof of Financing ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Proof of Financing")
If your home country is outside the EU, the German Embassy usually demands a proof of financing before issuing any VISA. In order to proof that you can finance your studies an amount of 934€ per month is required.
Please find further information on the proof of financing here:
[Study in Germany](https://www.study-in-germany.de/en/plan-your-studies/requirements/proof-of-financing/)
[DAAD Study and Research in Germany](https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/plan-your-studies/costs-of-education-and-living/)
[ Immigration Office ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Immigration Office")
In case you should need to apply for residence permit or need to extend your VISA you will have to arrange an appointment with the Immigartion Office of the city of Hannover. Appointments are usually booked up quickly. Therefore please do check entry regulations and VISA requirements carefully and book your appointment, if needed, well in advance.
Please find further information here:
[Immigration Office](https://www.hannover.de/en/view/content/703927/full/0/849516)
[ Health insurance and other insurances ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Health insurance and other insurances")
Sufficient health insurance is mandatory in Germany and is an essential prerequisite for your stay. A proof of sufficient coverage must be provided when applying for VISA and/or in order to enrol at Hochschule Hannover. Therefore please read the following information carefully and make sure that your health insurance provides sufficient coverage:
[Study in Germany](https://www.study-in-germany.de/en/plan-your-studies/requirements/health-insurance/)
[DAAD Study and Research in Germany](https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/plan-your-studies/health-insurance/)
Please do also consider a liability insurance.
There will be no enrolment without proof of sufficient health coverage. In order to enrol at Hochschule Hannover a digital insurance notification is needed (M10). You will have to apply for a digital insurance notification at a German statutory health insurance company. Please contact a health insurance company and ask for the notification needed.
The health insurance company then contacts Hochschule Hannover directly and informs about your insurance status.
The sender number of the Hochschule Hannover is: **H0000380**
German statutory health insurance companies are responsible for issuing certificates of health insurance coverage and for all issues related to compulsory health insurance for students. Please find information on
<https://www.krankenkassen.de/>
[Studentenwerk Hannover](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/internationale-studierende/erste-schritte-in-deutschland)
[ Finding accommodation ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Finding accommodation")
Student dormitories are managed by the Studentenwerk Hannover. Rooms there are only available for degree-seeking students, enrolled in a full-time bachelor’s or master’s degree study program at a university in Hannover. Please find further information concerning student housing at the dormitories managed by the Studentenwerk Hannover here:
[Studentenwerk Hannover](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/wohnungssuche)
We do support incoming exchange students in finding accommodation. Unfortunately Hochschule Hannover can only provide a limited number of rooms at different student dormitories in the city of Hannover. Incoming exchange students interested in accommodation at a student dormitory must apply for a room through the Incoming Team.
Since there is only a limited number of rooms available, we strongly recoomed to for alternative options. The Studentenwerk Hannover informs about private housing options here:
[Studentenwerk Hannover](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/wohnen/available-rooms/overview)
Please do check the following websites and/or platforms for housing options too:

[ Enrolment at Hochschule Hannover ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Enrolment at Hochschule Hannover")
All students are asked to pay the semester fee. Please note that this is not a tuition fee! It is a contribution covering administration and other student services. It finances numerous facilities of the student service organisation „Studentenwerk“, such as canteens, student dormitories, day care centers as well as the student union (AStA). It also includes the cost of the semester ticket.
[ ECTS Learning Agreement ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "ECTS Learning Agreement")
Before the start of the semester, students are asked to prepare a Learning Agreement. Please find the course offer at the different faculties of the Hochschule Hannover here:
[Course Program](https://typo3backend-live.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/exchange-students/courses-offered-for-exchange-students)
All faculties have appointed contact persons responsible for signing Learning Agreements. You may find the relevant contact data at the bottom of this page.
## During your stay
[ Citizen Office ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Citizen Office")
Citizens moving to Hannover, EU citizens as well as non-EU citizens, have to register at the Citizen Office of the city of Hannover. Please make sure to:
  * Register within 2 weeks after your arrival in Hannover
  * Bring along your passport, and the document "Wohnungsgeberbestätigung“, a confirmation of your landlord that you have moved into the apartment and have signed an rental contract
  * Schedule an appointment online with the citizen office


Appointments at the citizen office can be booked online:
[Citizen Office](https://e-government.hannover-stadt.de/online-terminvergabe/index.php?company=stadt-hannover&step=1&time=&zf=1d33795677f69698a1ef712030537e64)
A draft of the form "Wohnungsgeberbestätigung" can be found here:
[Form](https://serviceportal.hannover-stadt.de/downloads/datei/MTIxNTstOy92YXIvd3d3L2h0bWwvaGFubm92ZXIvbWVkaWVuL2Rva3VtZW50ZS93b2hudW5nc2dlYmVyYmVzdGFldGlndW5nLnBkZg%3D%3D)
[ IT Account ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "IT Account")
All students enroled at Hochschule Hannover receive a student account and an email address. With this account IT services such as WIFI (eduroam), the learning platform moodle are available as well as access to PC’s and printers on campus. Individual user names and passwords will be issued by the IT Service Department. Information concerning courses, exams and events during the semester will be sent to your student email address. Therefore please do make sure to check your emails.
Further information on the IT Services available as well as an explanation how to log into the WIFI network on campus can be found here:
[IT Services](https://service.it.hs-hannover.de/de/willkommen/)
[ Transcript of Records ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/internationales/wege-an-die-hochschule-hannover/austauschstudierende/den-aufenthalt-planen "Transcript of Records")
At the end of your stay the faculty will issue a Transcript of Records stating the courses you completed during your stay and the ECTS you achieved.
All faculties have appointed contact persons responsible for Learning Agreements and Transcripts of Records. You may find the relevant contact data at the bottom of this page.
