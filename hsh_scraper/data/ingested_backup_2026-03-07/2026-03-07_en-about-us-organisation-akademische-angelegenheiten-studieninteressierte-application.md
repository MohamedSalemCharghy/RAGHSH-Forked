#  Welcome to the Hochschule Hannover - University of Applied Sciences and Arts 
## Application portal
We are pleased that you have decided to study at the Hochschule Hannover - University of Applied Sciences and Arts (HsH). Here you can find out how to apply and what to look out for when applying.
Please note the application deadlines for the [individual degree courses](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/studienangebot/courses-from-a-z), since some courses start in the summer semester and some only in the winter semester. Likewise, different degree courses have a separate application deadline.
Before you apply for a place, you should familiarise yourself with the admission regulations for the respective degree course to check whether you meet the admission requirements.
You can find the admission regulations for your desired degree course on the website of the respective faculty under your desired degree course as well as on the page Laws and Ordinances.
