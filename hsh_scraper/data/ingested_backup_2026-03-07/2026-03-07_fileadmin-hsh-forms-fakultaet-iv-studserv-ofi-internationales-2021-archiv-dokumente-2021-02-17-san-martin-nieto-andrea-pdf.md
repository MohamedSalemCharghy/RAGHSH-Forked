Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
Studentenwohnheim, Studentenwerk.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
New Townhall, because is one of the most beautiful and
powerful places I have ever visited.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and why?**
Definitely yes, because this university teaches differently
than ours in Spain, we did many practical activities and I
think that this is the better way to learn.


**What were you surprised about, in regard to**
**stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
They are hard workers, shy and keep distances at the
beginning, but I realised that sometimes this is a
stereotype, because I discovered when they know about
the origin of the person and they are not from Germany,
German people try to help you and they are more patient.
They appreciate when people try to speak in German
because they know that it is extremely difficult.


**What did you like about studying at Hannover**
**university?**
The method they have it is really interesting, especially
for foreigners. they give you different ways to do the
activities, to practice and also, they give you the
possibility to develop in groups your personal skills. As I
said before, this is really enjoyable for us. Because in
Spain our method is more theoretical than practical.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so why/If not why not?**
I think so, after my university program, I decided to stay
longer, and I studied two German courses at the VHS
while I started working as a waitress, to earn some
money for my costs. My experience was really grateful so
maybe I will come back in the future.


**What 3 key things will you take away with you from**
**your studies in Hannover?**
Hardwork, Teamwork and constancy.
