Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
First, I was in a shared flat and then in a student residence.


**Which was your favorite place in Hannover and why?**
No doubt, Maschsee and Teich, because I love nature!

**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Studying, partying, making new friends.

**What** **were** **you** **surprised** **about, in** **regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
I've always been told that Germans are very correct, polite,
friendly and somehow cold, cold I mean distant. When I
was in Germany, I realised that people don’t lie. But this is
the culture of this country, we have to accept it, and if we
want to integrate into it, we have to get used to these
habits.

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
By all means yes.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Language

- Experiences

- Erasmus friends

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
I know it's going to sound like a joke, but my lasting
impression of Hannover is the view on the rooftop of
Hanover City Hall.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch**
**with when you go home?**
Yes


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, because I'm very fond of this city.
