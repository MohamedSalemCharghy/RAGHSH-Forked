Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
Heideviertel, it’s a single apartment on the second floor
with the landlords on the ground floor, provided by
Housing HSH.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Kröpcke Clock, because it is the place where every time
me and my friends meet before going somewhere else. It
is the beginning of everything.


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
I remember the first day welcome event and buffet, tour
in the city and Karaoke night in the Dublin Inn. Since
that’s the first event we group of international students
get to know each other.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Going to gym, or sometime go to explore the city with my
friends, sometime also staying home and cooking. During
the night maybe go to different parties with other
international students.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Absolutely yes. The Erasmus in HsH has been a unique
experience in life, and the fact of being able to know the
other people from different country is also important for
the future prospective. The equipment for learning in HsH
is well organized, and Hannover is a really nice city and
safe city to live this experience.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover? For example; the professors, the courses,**
**the equipment etc..**
I really like the fact that the class is really small, so that
everyone has the opportunity to properly be guided by
the professors. The work load is really low comparing to
our university, so that we can finally be able to do other
activities, or explore other topic.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Yes, the fact of knowing I was going to study aboard
pushed me to start to study German, which I continued
even after Erasmus. It makes Germany the second
country in Europe that I have ever visited after Italy.
Thanks to this experience, I had a better knowledge
about German culture, and German way of doing, which
somehow pushed me to decide to start my current
internship in Stuttgart.

Incoming-Interview


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
German culture and language
A more open mind with international way of thinking
A unique memory


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
A small, cozy and clean city, in which you will always find fun thing to do, if with right company.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes, all my Italian friends from same university, and some friends from Spain, we are still in contact.
One of them became my flat mate in Milan, and other two of them became my project group member
after Erasmus.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
I actually already went back once due to the visa and resident permit issue, and yes, I will go back for
sure, hopefully with all my friends I have made in Hannover.
