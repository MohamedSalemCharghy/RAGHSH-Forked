Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
Studentenwohnheim Bischofsholer Damm.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover? And**
**why?**
My favourite place was the Maschsee, because it was a
wonderful place to go with your friends.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and why?**
Yes, I would recommend it.


**What was your favourite subject and why?**
German language because I learned a lot, and I had a
great time with my classmates.


**What were you surprised about, in regard to**
**stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
I realized that not everyone is blond with blue eyes.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Yes.


**What 3 key things will you take away with you from**
**your studies in Hannover?**
New friends, autonomy and German language.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
One of the best experiences of my life.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
Yes.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so why/If not why not?**
Yes, because I want to see a lot of people that are
currently there.


**What did you like about studying at Hannover**
**university?**
What I liked most was the equipment.

**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
I didn’t have a buddy, so my teachers were the persons
who helped me.
