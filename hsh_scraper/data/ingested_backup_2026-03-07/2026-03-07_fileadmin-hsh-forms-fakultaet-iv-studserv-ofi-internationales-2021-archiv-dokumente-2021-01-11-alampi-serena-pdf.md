Incoming-Interview



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
My favourite place was Georgengarten, because I loved
how the nature is integrated in the city, and I loved
meeting with my friends there and doing picnics.


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
Probably the introduction week offered by Hochschule
Hannover was one of the best parts of my experience. I
absolutely recommend it. We explored the city, and we
did a lot of activities, such as ice skating, karaoke or visit
museums. The most important part, in my opinion, is that
we had the occasion to create a strong group of
international students. We became friends, and friends
are very important for this kind of experience.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
In my free time I always met with my friends, to explore
and visit both new cities, or Hannover itself.

**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Yes, I would recommend the experience in Hochschule
Hannover because it is an occasion to improve yourself,
both from the study point of view, and the
life/responsibility one.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
I was surprised that I found so many kind people ready to
help me.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

I liked the courses and the professors, because they
were always available for any help.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
I really liked Germany, and I’d like to learn the language
even better, so maybe one day I will move to Germany
again. Since I liked the experience, I also decided to do
another exchange, but in another country.

Incoming-Interview


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
Responsibility
Friends
Professional learnings


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
A city where you can find anything you need, and that will always contain a lot of memories.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes.

**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, I think I will come back to Hannover one day. I can’t wait to be again in this city that is full of
beautiful memories.
