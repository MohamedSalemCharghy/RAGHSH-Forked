# Study programmes
We offer Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes, part-time and dual study programmes. In addition to engineering, economics and social sciences, Hannover University of Applied Sciences and Arts also offers a wide range of media and creative study programmes of high, internationally renowned educational quality. See what best suits your interests and career aspirations. 
What might also be of interest to you: by integrating practical phases into your studies, you can gain professional experience and establish contacts with companies during your time at university. Innovative degree programme concepts, small study groups and an overall very application-oriented university education mean that you are ideally prepared for the world of work.
If you have any questions about study programs or orientation, don't hesitate to reach out to our [Student Advisory Service](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/studienangebot/courses-from-a-z)!
