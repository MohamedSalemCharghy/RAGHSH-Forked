###### Nine degree programs The Faculty V – Diaconic Studies, Health Care and Social Work offers six Bachelor degree and three Master degree programs all of which feature a tight link between theory and practice and an interdisciplinary approach. ###### Inclusive Education

The ongoing discussion on inclusion in theory and practice and the strive for
rethinking and rebuilding educational to inclusive settings have challenged
the field of inclusive education. Theory and practice of inclusive education
aim at supporting the full participation of children, young people and adults
in all parts of society, in particular in education, training, leisure, housing and
work. In line with Article 3 of the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with
Disabilities, the study program will enable students to conduct theory-based
conception, implementation and evaluation of those professional measures
that contribute to the full participation and inclusion of people with disabilities
in society. State recognition is granted after completion of the work placement
and a final examination.

###### Inclusive Education (job-integrated)

The job-integrated Bachelor degree program Inclusive Education offers
students the opportunity to integrate their study into their professional life.
At the beginning, this five-semester study program introduces students to
working and thinking in an academic manner. Four semesters are assigned to
different themes of inclusive education with a main focus on reducing barriers
and increasing participation at all levels of interaction, organization, and in
society. The fifth semester prepares students for writing their final thesis in a
supervised Bachelor colloquium.


Classes take place Mondays during spring term (mid March to beginning of July)
and fall term (mid September to beginning of January). In addition, there is a
block week at the beginning and end of each term and three days every semester
for mentoring group meetings. Block weeks are eligible for paid educational
leave (Bildungsurlaub) according to the Educational Leave Law of Lower Saxony.

###### Nursing (extra-occupational)


The Bachelor degree program in Nursing qualifies graduates to independently carry out complex activities in health care facilities. The program features
three major fields of study in the second part of the program:

- Patient and family counseling (applied nursing science)

- Teaching and training conception (educational sciences)

- Organization and management.


This degree program offers courses parallel to vocational training and professional occupation. Students with higher education entrance qualifications from the currently nine partner schools in the fields of health care and
nursing, pediatric nursing care and care of the elderly have the possibility
to participate in the first part of the study (1st – 5th pre-semester over 2.5
years) parallel to their vocational training. In these years students attend
courses at the Hochschule Hannover, take examinations and, thus, acquire
necessary ECTS credits for the second part of the study (4th – 7th semester).

###### Religious Education and Social Work

This eight-semester Bachelor degree program combines contents and
perspectives of two fields of study – protestant religious education and social
work. With its comparative approach it identifies similarities and differences,
interfaces and potential collaborations in the fields of religious and Christian
educational, diaconal and social work. The degree program qualifies both,
for the traditional fields of institution-related clerical educational work and
Diaconia/Social Welfare, and for activities in the fields of social work. In
addition to the modules for the BA social work degree, the program offers
modules preparing for a religious profession. These include, apart from
theological classes, fields of church-related practice, pastoral care studies and
religious education. A one-year work experience follows the Bachelor degree
program. Afterwards, graduates can acquire the ecclesiastical professional
recognition as a deacon (in case of protestant religious affiliation) and a state
recognition as a social worker.

###### Social Work

The Bachelor degree program in Social Work enables students to work in a
wide range of social work settings (e.g. child and youth welfare, school social
work, psychiatry, or ex-offender assistance). Graduates are able to critically
analyse social problems and their causes and to develop and implement
methodologically sound interventions. The study program qualifies for all
occupational fields and requirements of social work and opens manifold
employment opportunities in public service institutions, welfare organizations
and associations as well as private sector social organizations.


As an independent action science, social work integrates problem-oriented
elements of other sciences and pursues the goal of comprehensive political,
social and cultural empowerment. These aspects are reflected in the curriculum
of the degree program which comprises a standard period of study of 6
semesters. For justified reasons (e.g., employment, family issues) the study
can be completed on a part-time basis. In this case, the standard period of
study is extended accordingly.


A one-year work experience follows the study in which graduates are employed
full-time at institutions of social work. At the workplace they are supervised
by experienced social workers and are also accompanied by teachers of the
Hochschule on regular study days. State recognition is granted after completion
of the work placement and a final examination.

##### Social Work (extra-occupational)

The extra-occupational Bachelor degree program in Social Work enables students to work in a wide range of fields of social work, to reflect and to keep
up with recent developments. The program takes into account existing experiences of students in the fields of social work. It is specially designed for
people already involved in these fields of work, who, however, lack required
qualification.


The study qualifies for all fields of work and requirements of social work
and opens a variety of employment opportunities in social work institutions,
social services and public authorities.

###### Education Sciences and Management for Nursing and Health Care professionals (extra-occupational)

The Master program offers students after completion of a relevant Bachelor
degree program two elective key study areas for future profession. Majoring in
Education Sciences qualifies students for an independent teaching activity as
well as the administration of schools of public health, and, at the same time,
provides access to state vocational schools. Majoring in Management qualifies
for superior management functions in the health care system, as well as for
cross-company organizations and management in the health care system.

###### Social Work (MSW)


A six-semester Master program in Social Work is designed for graduates of
Social Work or comparable Bachelor or German Diplom degree programs. The
Master program is focused on social work science and practice research with
a concentration on social theories, social work science, research methodology
and evaluation (among other topics and fields). By working on relevant topics
and issues in their research projects students contribute to the expansion of
professional practice already during their studies. The Master degree enables
graduates to attain access to senior positions, teaching, planning, counseling
and research tasks as well as to PhD studies.

###### Therapeutic Work with Children and Adolescents

The Master program in Therapeutic Work with Children and Adolescents
focuses primarily on clinical practice in dealing with mentally ill children and
adolescents. The degree program is offered in cooperation with the Winnicott
Institute of Hannover. Program content complies with the requirements for
exercising the profession as children‘s and youth psychotherapist regulated by
the law on psychotherapy. The study program allows students to explore and
deal with extensive interdisciplinary subjects and neighboring disciplines,
especially with development and attachment research, neuroscience, biology,
social sciences and educational sciences. Attainment of a Master of Arts
degree allows graduates to pursue further studies at PhD level.

###### International orientation

The Faculty V is an internationally oriented faculty. International cooperations,
student and teaching staff mobility (incoming and outgoing) and “internationalization
at home” are key points in the international implementation plan of the Hochschule
Hannover and the Faculty V.

###### Teaching and staff mobility, research opportunities


Academic, administrative and management staff from partner and non-partner
universities are welcome to visit the Faculty V for teaching, research and training
purposes as well as to exchange ideas and best-practice examples. Teaching
exchange is possible in various disciplines, such as social work, religious
education, health care and inclusive education. Members of the Faculty V are
also open for joint international research projects and publications. To arrange
teaching, research and staff mobility, please contact the International Coordinator
and/or the International Faculty Office of the Faculty V.

###### Courses for exchange students


The Faculty V offers exchange students an opportunity to attend courses held either
in German or in English. Exchange students with a good command of German can
attend courses within different fields of study (social work, religious education
and social work, inclusive education).


For English speaking students, the Faculty V designed a number of courses within
the Bachelor degree program in Social Work, which are taught predominantly in
English. Some of the practical workshops are held both in English and in German.
Exchange students can acquire the required ECTS credits for a semester abroad
by attending these courses.


Moreover, exchange students have the possibility to attend courses beyond their
field of study, such as language courses, courses on intercultural communication
and on presentation techniques, as well as a study camp and spring welcome
weeks (orientation weeks in fall and spring terms) with an integrated intensive
German course.


Course catalogs are subject to modifications. Updated course catalogs for a
respective semester with a general description of the courses are available on the
website of the Faculty V. 19

###### Assistance for international students

The assistance service of the International Faculty Office of the Faculty V
provides international students with all relevant details about how and what
they can study at the Hochschule Hannover. Here students get information and
advice on the structure, content, Learning Agreement and any other study-related and organizational questions.


The International Coordinator advises students regarding what classes to
take, how to structure their study, how many ECTS credit points they need to
acquire, and helps find work placements.


In addition, there is an Incoming-Team at the Student Center of the Hochschule Hannover that assists international students in all questions around
application, visa, housing, health insurance, study fees, CampusCard, etc.
For inquiries please mail to: hsh-incoming@hs-hannover.de

###### Semester fee


The Hochschule Hannover charges a semester fee of approximately 350 euros. The semester fees are contributions to support certain student services
such as dormitories, meals at the dining halls and administrative costs for
the Student Council. By paying the semester fee, students can buy meals at
the dining halls at a reduced price and get a semester ticket, which allows
free use of local public transportation in the Hannover region and numerous
regional trains (but not ICE and IC) throughout Lower-Saxony.

##### Hochschule Hannover

###### With 61 degree programs at five faculties, the Hochschule Hannover (HsH) is one of the largest universities of applied sciences and arts in Lower Saxony, Germany. The HsH edu- cation focuses on practical, scientifically sound degree pro- grams with international orientation. Moreover, numerous dual and part-time extra-occupational study programs are offered at the HsH. The Faculty V – Diaconic Studies, Health Care and Social Work is located on the Campus Kleefeld of the Hochschule Hannover. More than 1,400 students are enrolled at the fa- culty employing around 40 professors, as well as numerous contracted teachers and full-time lecturers. The Faculty V emerged from the former Protestant University of Applied Sciences founded in 1971. Please find more information about the HsH on our website: www.hs-hannover.de
