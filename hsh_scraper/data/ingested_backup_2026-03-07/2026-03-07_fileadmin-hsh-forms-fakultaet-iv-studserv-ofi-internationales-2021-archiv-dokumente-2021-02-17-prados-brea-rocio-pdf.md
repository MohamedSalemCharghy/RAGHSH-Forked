Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
I was living in Bischofsholer Damm. It is a student
residence of Studentenwerk.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover? And**
**why?**
I really do not have a favourite place there because I
really love everything in Hannover. But I used to take a
walk around the Maschsee, and I loved this place.


**3. What did you do in Induction Week and would you**
**recommend it?**
Hochschule Hannover has a great Introduction Week. On
my first day there I met a lot of people from all around the
world, and I met Spanish people who nowadays are my
friends. We went to a Karaoke Bar, drank a lot of beers,
etc. I really recommend it, because you can meet people
of many nationalities and know their cultures.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I went go-karting, took a walk around Hauptbahnhof,
went shopping, and visited different cities in Germany
and abroad.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and why?**
Of course, I really recommend Hannover Hochschule.
They offer good courses for exchange students, and they
have good professors.


**What was your favourite subject and why?**
My favourite subject was ICM. It is an intercultural
experience. We were living for a week with people of
many cultures, and I learnt so much about coexistence.


**Which transport did you use to come to the**
**university and to travel around the city?**
Hannover has a great transport network. I took the U-Ban
to go to university or go to my friends’ houses.


**What were you surprised about, in regard to**
**stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
In the beginning, German people are shy and distant, but
when you really meet them they are so funny, and good
friends.

Incoming-Interview


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around the university, and did the same person**
**help you also in daily life, or someone different?**
The buddy program is so useful. With them you know you are not alone in your new experience at
another university. My buddy was Jasmin, and she spoke Spanish that made me feel more comfortable
in the first days. But in the Hochschule everyone is so nice, and you really can ask everything to
everyone. I had classmates like Franky, or teachers like Patricia or Henning, who are always ready to
help you.


**What did you like about studying at Hannover university?**
I think the professors are the best in Hochschule. Patricia Adam and Henning Austmann are the best
professors I have, not only in Hannover but in my university life.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
My experience of living abroad changed my point of view about everything.


**What 3 key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
Meeting a lot of people.
Speaking a little bit of German.
Learning a lot of things about the business.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
Hannover is a beautiful place for living and studying.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Of course, I have it. I met a lot of people there who I hope to have with me all my life.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so why/If not why not?**
I am sure I will go back to Hannover, because I have friends living there, and I really have beautiful
memories there that I would like to remember.
