Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
I lived in two different student appartments while I lived
and studied in Hannover. One in Linden and the other in
Südstadt, and personally, I think these are very nice
areas to live in.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
I loved many things about places in Hannover: The
houses and their lovely architecture, the food locals in
Limmerstrasse, Eilenriede Park, Herrenhäuser Garten (I
especially liked this small “room” with mosaics and
figures created by Niki de Saint Phalle, “Grotte
Herrenhausen”), the New Rathaus, the beautiful
decoration of the city during Christmas, walking through
the streets of Südstadt, the Marienburg Castle, exploring
Linden, the parks, the lakes… I loved all of these places
because they are different from what my home city and
country have.

**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I would go with my friends and take the train to travel to
different towns and cities nearby, using our student
transport card. We went to Hamburg, Bremen, Hameln,
Lübeck, Lüneburg (and saw the beach for the first time in
Germany!), Celle, etc. in Lower Saxony, but we also
organized trips to other cities in Germany and other
countries.

We would go to Bowling World Hannover or the Dublin
Inn Karaoke nights on Monday. We would go to the fairs
and go on the attractions, to the museums, to the
Maschsee and have an ice cream while it was sunny; to
take the bike and discover new places or make a picnic
by the Rathaus or the lakes. We would enjoy the events
they prepared in the city whenever there was one
(concerts on the street, food fairs, the Festival of
Lights…), we would go to the barbeques of my faculty or
do a barbeque ourselves in the park in front of Leibniz
University… and much more! We even went to Hannover
HDI Arena once and saw Hannover96 play.

During the weekends we would go to Zaza, Indiego
Glocksee, Bei Chez Heinz, Havana… and party all night
☺

Incoming-Interview


**After your semester at Hannover University, would you recommend it to other students and if**
**so, why / if not, why not?**
Yes, I would. Professors are very nice and helpful, and lessons are dynamic and entertaining. The
installations of my faculty are good, lesson rooms are small, but we still managed to fit all, and when I
was there, they were making renovations, so I bet now it’s so much more improved. I liked so much the
opportunity HsH gave me with the free German lessons, because thanks to these lessons I discovered
my interest in this language, and now I’m still studying to improve my German skills! ☺

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
Business ethics & sustainable development from a management perspective, with Henning Austmann. I
liked this subject the most, because I’m interested in environmental and social issues. and the current
challenges that require action.

**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around the university, and did the same person**
**help you also in daily life, or someone different?**
I didn’t have any buddy, but the girls in the international department always helped me (and I constantly
asked them for help), and teachers, and colleagues… One day we needed help with something that had
nothing to do with the Hochschule, and Beate Yardim from the international department helped us by
being a German translator. That was so nice.


**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**

I perceive a more committed attitude towards tasks and exams in university. Here in Spain students are
also responsible, study hard and make an effort to get good marks, but I perceived German students to
be more involved with their assignments, and to make use of legit information in order to exchange
ideas, and come-up with solutions, instead of just making them to pass exams.

**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
Absolutely! When I moved back to Spain, I kept studying German and planned to move out again to
Germany in the future, to search for an internship or job in marketing (which is my field). For now, I am
in Spain gaining experience, as I wanted my first professional experience to take place in my home
country, but I will start searching for a job in Germany soon, to begin later this year or by the beginning
of 2021.

**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
The mind opening experience, the importance of meeting new cultures, and learning about different
nationalities for your self-growth, and the benefits of studying abroad to become a more mature, tolerant
and independent person.

**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, I will! Whether just travelling and come back with my friends, or to study or do an internship when
my German is good enough.

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
That it will always stay in my heart and that it gave me the best year of my life.
