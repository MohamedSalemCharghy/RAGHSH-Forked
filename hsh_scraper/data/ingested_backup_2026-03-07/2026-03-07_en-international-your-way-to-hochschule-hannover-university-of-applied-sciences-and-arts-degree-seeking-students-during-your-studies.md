[ What do I have to do when my residence permit/transitional certificate has expired? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "What do I have to do when my residence permit/transitional certificate has expired?")
Before your residence permit/transitional certificate expires, contact the Foreigners Office by phone (Monday-Thursday from 08:00 - 16:00 and Friday from 08:00 - 14:00) or by e-mail.
Please keep in mind that due to the high number of inquiries, it may take several weeks to process your request.
So be sure to contact them in time (preferably a few weeks before expiration) to avoid stress when extending your stay.
[ Am I allowed to work with my residence permit/transitional certificate? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "Am I allowed to work with my residence permit/transitional certificate?")
This usually results from the ancillary provisions of your residence title (e.g., from the supplementary sheet). If these contain a permission for employment, you are allowed to work.
[ My electronic residence permit (eAT) has been applied for. How long does it take to process? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "My electronic residence permit \(eAT\) has been applied for. How long does it take to process?")
The current processing time takes 6-8 weeks. As soon as the electronic residence title has been received by the Foreigners Office, it will be sent promptly to your specified registration address. If you have received a letter from the Federal Printing Office, there is no need for you to take any action.
[ My residence permit has expired. I would like to travel. What do I have to do? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "My residence permit has expired. I would like to travel. What do I have to do?")
If your residence title has expired, it is not possible to travel abroad.
It is also not possible to leave the country with a temporary certificate.
In this case, the usual procedure as described under "What do I have to do if my residence permit/transitional certificate has expired?" must be followed. In urgent cases, the residence permit can be issued as a sticker.
[ I am a foreign student in Hannover. Which documents do I need for the extension? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "I am a foreign student in Hannover. Which documents do I need for the extension?")
As a rule, the Foreigners Office requires a current certificate of enrollment, from the 4th semester onwards a current proof of academic achievement (transcript of records), proof of health insurance, proof that the student's livelihood is secured (e.g., a blocked account). Additional documentation may be required in individual cases.
In the case of doctoral students, the Foreigners Office always requires a current status of the doctorate from the doctoral advisor.
The securing of the livelihood can be proven by a blocked account, a declaration of commitment, a scholarship as well as an employment contract. A combination of the individual points is also possible.
Application forms, checklist and further information can be found [here](https://www.hannover.de/Leben-in-der-Region-Hannover/Verwaltungen-Kommunen/Die-Verwaltung-der-Landeshauptstadt-Hannover/Dezernate-und-Fachbereiche-der-LHH/Finanzen,-Ordnung-und-Feuerwehr/Fachbereich-%C3%96ffentliche-Ordnung/Standesamt-und-Staatsangeh%C3%B6rig%C2%ADkeit/Ausl%C3%A4nderangelegen%C2%ADheiten-und-Staatsangeh%C3%B6rigkeit/Kundenservice-f%C3%BCr-Fachkr%C3%A4fte,-Studierende-und-Selbstst%C3%A4ndige-sowie-deren-Familienangeh%C3%B6rige/Formulare).
[ Do I need a visa to enter the federal territory? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies " Do I need a visa to enter the federal territory?")
The mandatory requirement for the issuance of a residence permit is the entry with a required visa.
The visa requirement of a foreigner must first be assessed on the basis of Regulation (EU) 2018/1806 (EU-VisumVO).
According to Article 3 (1) of the EU Visa Regulation, nationals of third countries listed in Annex I to the EU Visa Regulation are subject to the visa requirement.
Pursuant to Art. 4 (1) EU-VisaVO, third-country nationals listed in Annex II are exempt from the visa requirement for short-term stays of up to 90 days during a reference period of 180 days and without gainful employment.
**Specialty:**
Pursuant to Section 41 (1) of the Residence Ordinance, nationals of Australia, Israel, Japan, Canada, the Republic of Korea, New Zealand, and the United States of America may enter and reside in the territory of the Federal Republic of Germany without a visa even for a stay that is not a short stay. A required residence permit can be obtained in the federal territory.
The same applies pursuant to Section 41 (2) of the Residence Ordinance to nationals of Andorra, Brazil, El Salvador, Honduras, Monaco, and San Marino who do not wish to engage in gainful employment other than the activities specified in Section 17 (2).

[ What is a "Fiktionsbescheinigung"? Can I travel abroad with it? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "What is a "Fiktionsbescheinigung"? Can I travel abroad with it?")
A "Fiktionsbescheinigung" is generally issued if the Foreigners Office cannot decide on the application for issuance/extension because, for example, documents are missing. This results from the wording of Section 81 (3) and (4) of the Residence Act. The residence is considered as continuing until the Foreigners Office has decided on the application.
**Permission fiction ("Erlaubnisfiktion"):**
§ Section 81 paragraph.3 sentence 1 of the Residence Act reads:
"(3) 1 If a foreigner who is lawfully residing in the Federal territory without possessing a residence title applies for the issuance of a residence title, his or her residence shall be deemed permitted until the decision of the aliens authority."
This is a so-called "permission fiction". It applies to foreigners who are allowed to obtain a residence title without a visa (e.g., US-Americans). A re-entry into the federal territory is not possible.
**Continuance fiction ("Fortbestandsfiktion"):**
§ Section 81(4) sentence 1 Residence Act reads:
"(4) 1 If a foreigner applies for the renewal of his or her residence title or for the issuance of another residence title before its expiration, the previous residence title shall be deemed to continue to exist from the time of its expiration until the decision of the Foreigners Office."
This is the so-called "fiction of continued existence". It applies to foreigners who already have an eAT in the Federal Republic and have applied for the extension of their residence title.

[ What do I have to do if my residence card has been stolen or I have lost my card? ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/international/your-way-to-hochschule-hannover-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/degree-seeking-students/during-your-studies "What do I have to do if my residence card has been stolen or I have lost my card?")
The first thing to do is to inform the **police** by filing a **report of loss (Verlustmeldung)**. 
You must also report the loss/theft to the relevant **foreigner’s authority (Ausländerbehörde)**. They will report the residence card to the **Federal Police** for tracing and apply a **passport transfer** **(Passübertrag)**. This means that a new card will be ordered - you must still report the loss/theft separately to the police. You must bring your passport and a biometric passport photo to the appointment at the foreigner’s authority (Ausländerbehörde). It is best if you ask the staff before which documents you need to bring with you.

## Financing
For international students in particular, the question of financing their studies is a major challenge. In most cases, they are denied access to BAföG.
Here we present options for financing your studies.
### General information
The Hannover Science Initiative provides explanatory videos on the topic of student financing that will help you get an initial overview of your options:
[Video: How to finance my studies in Germany?](https://wissen.hannover.de/Studieren/Studienstart/Alles-zum-Thema-Studienfinanzierung)
The so called Studentenwerk Hannover also has a lot of important and helpful information for you on its website:
[Website: Studentenwerk Hannover, Financing](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/en/internationale-studierende/vor-dem-studium)
### Jobbing
In most cases it is not possible to finance a stay without a student job. In Hannover and at the Hochschule Hannover University of Applied Sciences and Arts there is a large offer of student jobs.
The Initiative “Initiative Wissenschaft Hannover" also offers a helpful video on this topic:
[Video: International students and jobs](https://wissen.hannover.de/Studieren/International/Ausl%C3%A4ndische-Studierende-und-Jobben)
The so called “Studentenwerk” Hannover has also compiled further information and contacts for you [here](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/internationale-studierende/standard-titel).
### Scholarships
Another possibility to finance your studies, at least partially, are scholarships.
The “Initiative Wissenschaft Hannover” has also created an [overview of scholarship opportunities](https://www.hannover.de/Wirtschaft-Wissenschaft/Wissenschaft/Studieren-in-Hannover/Internationales/Stipendien-und-Unterst%C3%BCtzung).
At Hochschule Hannover University of Applied Sciences and Arts, so called “WIR/Deutschland” scholarships are also awarded annually to students at the university. [Here](https://www.hs-hannover.de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsh/rund-ums-studium/stipendien/wir-stipendium-studierende/) you can find more information about the WIR/Deutschland STIPENDIUM.
Another option is the [Landesstipendium Niedersachsen](https://www.hs-hannover.de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsh/rund-ums-studium/stipendien/landesstipendium-niedersachsen/). This is a one-time scholarship in the amount of 500.00 euros. (If you apply for the WIR scholarship, this application also counts for an application for the Landesstipendium Niedersachsen).
For international students, scholarships are also awarded each year at HsH in cooperation with the DAAD for special commitment or as graduation assistance. We share information about this via our Moodle group for international students and social media, as well as via our website.

## Accommodation
There are many private and public student residences in Hannover. Nevertheless, it is not always easy for students to find suitable living space. Especially before the semester starts, many students are looking for a room or an apartment.
The "Initiative Wissenschaft Hannover” has created an explanatory video for initial orientation, which you can access [here](https://wissen.hannover.de/Studieren/International/Ausl%C3%A4ndische-Studierende-und-Wohnen).
Furthermore, the so called Studentenwerk Hannover has useful information about the [student housing](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/wohnen/wohnhaeuser/) and offers a [housing exchange.](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/index.php?id=265)
**Rental costs**
Monthly room rates range from €300 to €550; for a complete apartment, you should expect a much higher rent.
**Rental contracts**
There are binding rental agreements, which means that by signing them you confirm that you are a tenant. Rental agreements can be mutually cancelled only if they are terminated 3 months in advance.
As a rule, you must pay a so-called security deposit. It is a security for the landlord. If the apartment or room was not left clean or facilities were damaged, then the landlord keeps all or part of the deposit. The deposit is also used for renting defaults.

## Psychological counseling service for students (ptb)
Studying can be a challenge. Whether you are at the start of your studies or almost at the end: The psychological counselling service offers free advice concerning problems caused by studying as well as in personal conflict situations.
You can turn to our experienced counsellors to talk about problems such as:
  * crises and disturbances in the course of your studies such as test anxiety, learning and achievement disorders
  * difficulties with personal relations like family or friends
  * problems ranging from loneliness and isolation with depressive moods or suicidal thoughts
  * difficulties adjusting to new circumstances/culture/country


Further information you can find here: <https://www.ptb.uni-hannover.de/en/>
