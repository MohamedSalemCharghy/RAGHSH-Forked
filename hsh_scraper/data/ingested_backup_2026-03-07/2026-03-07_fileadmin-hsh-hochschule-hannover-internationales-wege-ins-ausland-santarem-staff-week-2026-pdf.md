Dear collegue,


You are warmly invited to join us in Santarém, Portugal, for the _**International Staff Week**_
_**2026 - “AI-Driven and Sustainable Futures for Higher Education”**_, hosted by ISLA Santarém Polytechnic University from 16–20 March 2026, featuring a keynote, hands-on workshops on
AI in teaching and learning, round tables and debates on European alliances, cooperation
and blended intensive programmes among others, an Academic Fair (“Your University
Represented”) and “Your University in 50 Seconds”, a full Culture Day in Santarém and the
Ribatejo’s region with lunch and joint dinner, and a relaxed farewell with reflections and
certificates; the working language is English and the event is aimed at academic and
administrative staff interested in AI, pedagogy, internationalisation and sustainable
institutional development.
