#  Courses offered for exchange students 
  

  
Discover a wide variety of courses across our five faculties:  
Electrical Engineering, Mechanical & Bioprocess Engineering, Media, Information & Design, Business & Computer Science, and Social Work.  
  
No matter your field of study, there is something here for you. To learn more about the courses available during your exchange semester at HsH, simply select your area of interest below and contact our International Faculty Officers.  
  
You can find their contact details at the bottom of the page.

Whether you wish to deepen your subject knowledge or gain international academic experience, our exchange programme provides excellent opportunities to engage with current developments in this innovative field.

## Contact Information: International Faculty Officers
Hochschule Hannover is committed to promoting and supporting international student mobility. Each faculty has appointed a contact person for exchange students - known as an**International Faculty Officer** - who assists you with academic matters related to your exchange.
If you have questions about the faculty **course offering for exchange students** , your **Learning Agreement** , **ECTS** or your **Transcript of Records** , please get in touch with the International Faculty Officer in your faculty of study. They will provide guidance on course selection and ensure that important documents, such as the Learning Agreement, are completed and finalised both internally and in agreement with your home university.
