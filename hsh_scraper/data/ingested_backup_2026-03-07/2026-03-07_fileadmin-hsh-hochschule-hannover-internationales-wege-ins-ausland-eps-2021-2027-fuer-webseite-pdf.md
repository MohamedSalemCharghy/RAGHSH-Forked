The ERASMUS+ Program is the core element of the Hochschule Hannover’s internationalization strategy. The
executive board of the university recently decided that there should be a regional focus on cooperation with partner
institutions in the European program countries. This decision has also been emphasized in recent negotiations with the
government. Thus, the participation in the ERASMUS+ Program and the regional focus on cooperation with European
partner institutions entered the target agreements negotiated between the university and the government. The target
agreements have officially been passed recently. The mobility of students and staff is highly recommended and
supported at the Hochschule Hannover. The university has established processes and measures to increase the
mobility of individuals in order to enhance international and intercultural experiences among students and staff.


The ERASMUS+ program is so much more than the mobility of individuals. As part of its internationalization strategy,
the Hochschule Hannover is aiming at using the ERASMUS+ program as a catalyst for internationalization,
modernization of the institution and its teaching as well as for the personal growth of its members – students and staff.


On an individual basis the objective of the University is to promote and enable mobility in order to follow the principals
of the European Education Area. Mobility should be seen as a core element in the student life cycle. In a global world
intercultural as well as fundamental linguistic skills in at least two modern languages should be the norm. A cultural
understanding of others, different principals and methods of teaching are indispensable elements in the education of
young individuals on their way to become European or even global citizens. It is the responsibility but also the challenge
of the educational institutions to provide the means to support the individuals in its care on this path. Needless to say,
this does not begin and end with student mobility. The same principle applies to the mobility of teaching and nonteaching staff because the internationalization of the institution is shaped by its individual stakeholders. It has to be
applied to the individual as well as to the university structures, teaching and learning methods as well as in its attitude
towards international elements and the development of key competences in a wide range of skills. Internationalization
is a cross-sectional task that needs to be or become a core element of the DNA of any university.


As a University of Applied Sciences and Arts there are certain areas that are a natural focus when we look closely at
our target groups, our set up as an educational institution and its scientific focus: these areas are mainly diversity,
inclusion and all the different areas of sustainability. As a University of Applied Sciences and Arts our student body and
staff are very diverse. A great number of our students are first generation academics, many come from an immigration
background, have a different mother tongue than German, have already a family, study part-time or study with certain
handicaps. Diversity is a daily topic at Hochschule Hannover. The challenge is to include the different target groups
into our internationalization strategy and to use the chances offered through the different dimensions of the ERASMUS+
program to their benefit. Furthermore, the topics of sustainability have already a tradition at Hochschule Hannover.
This applies to the wide range of the engineering sciences at Hochschule Hannover as well as to the areas of design
and media where sustainability is a strong focus area in the fashion department and the product design.


The philosophy of the Hochschule Hannover can best be described as such: the university provides the stem of the
tree of internationalization, the different topics and key players are represented by the branches while the ERAMSUS+
program provides the fertilizer so that the tree can grow and thrive.

Incoming students find all relevant information related to their stay abroad, such as organisational matters and
accommodation, on the website of the International Office. In addition to that there is additional information sent to all
applicants via email. The incoming student coordinator is available for further information and question in this regard.
The Hochschule Hannover has a cooperation with the student dormitories run by the government. There is a set number
of rooms at different student dormitories in the city of Hannover which is allocated to the International Office each
semester.

For international staff and guests, the staff mobility coordinator offers support in finding accommodation. For this
purpose, the Hochschule Hannover maintains cooperation with guest houses and landlords where guests of the
Hochschule Hannover can be accommodated. For short term incoming staff, the International Office offers to book
rooms at a local hotel at discounted rates. All students and staff can receive information and advice concerning
insurances. The necessity of appropriate insurance coverage is part of the Grant Agreement. Responsible staff at the
International Office also offers advice and support concerning visa.

In order to prepare the participants as good as possible, the different branches of the Hochschule Hannover are working
closely together in order to present preparation packages in language and intercultural preparation for students and
staff. For example, language courses tailored for outgoing as well as incoming mobilities are available as regular as
well as intensive courses during semester break or just before semester starts. Additional courses are available during
the entire semester accompanying the regular curriculum. Through the interactive platform “Wanderlust” students can
join a tandem program. Outgoing and incoming students join here to prepare themselves linguistically as well as
interculturally amongst their peers before their stay abroad. The institution is well aware of the responsibility to conduct
and offer courses aiming at the enhancement of language as well as international and intercultural skills. Therefore,
there is a regular offer of intercultural courses for students and staff, outgoing as well as incoming, to further enhance
their intercultural skills and to prepare for or accompany their mobility under professional supervision.

Staff mobilities are a core element of the professional development policy of the Hochschule Hannover. The
Hochschule Hannover recognises staff mobilities as an important part of its internationalization and modernization
strategy. International mobile staff is awarded a certificate when taking part in a preparatory language course, a course
in Intercultural Interaction Competence (IKHK) and realized an ERASMUS+ staff mobility.


Feedback from participants is welcomed, monitored and used to further improve the services offered. Participant
reports are being monitored and participants are invited and encouraged to share their experiences with the responsible
staff at the International Office. This feedback is also welcomed during information events to better inform and support
interested students and staff prior to their application.


Erasmus Key Action 2 (KA2) - Cooperation among organisations and institutions:


While the quantitative focus of Hochschule Hannover in the past has been in the key action KA1, there has been
individual involvement in Key Action 2 and 3 projects as well. In the years to come, there are already plans to be more
active in other key areas of the ERAMSUS+ program. As a basis we use the already existing infrastructure and
successful projects and partnerships with education institutions as well as companies outside the educational sector.


The double degree programs of different faculties with partner institutions in program as well as partner countries are
a successful basis to focus more on the involvement in strategic partnerships, ERASMUS Mundus Joint Master
Degrees as well as the initiative for European Universities. Hochschule Hannover is already active in different University
networks and is planning to take its focus of internationalization to the next level.


All actions planned and targeted will be adjusted to fit the framework of the installed infrastructure in internationalization
at Hochschule Hannover. As such, Hochschule Hannover accomplishes that communication of relevant information
and regulations is being distributed and the programs installed are being managed and supervised through the
established central project management.

The overall aim is to see the ERASMUS+ program in different parts and on different levels throughout the University.
In order to achieve this goal, it is necessary to promote the program constantly within the institution, to show its benefit
and possibilities to the stakeholders and individuals of the University in order to paint a clear picture of how the program
may be used for the benefit of the individual as well as the institution in general.

When it comes to qualitative and quantitative measures it is necessary to differentiate. The impact of participation is
often monitored in numbers. The target number of physical student mobility is set at 20%. In modern times we do have
to put this number into perspective. Physical mobility is important and should be accessible for all students and staff
no matter the subject, the destination, the family and/or financial background or certain predispositions. The University’s
goal is to have at least 20% of their students carrying out a physical mobility of a longer period.
At the same time institutions should consider other targets in order to internationalize their target groups. The promotion
and recognition of virtual mobility for example, shorter mobility periods, stronger involvement of the individuals in
concepts for an internationalization at home. All these activities serve also the purpose of a European Education Area
without necessarily the demand for a semester or year abroad.

In order to ensure the promotion and management under high quality standards, the Hochschule Hannover has
installed a full-time position for the position of the ERASMUS+ Institutional Coordinator. All communication, program
promotion as well as project management of all projects under the ERASMUS+ scheme are managed throughout this
position and its associates. Furthermore, in order to guarantee a fair and high-quality management of the mobility
programs, Hochschule Hannover introduced a fair, transparent and coherent selection process for all mobility programs
on a centralized basis. Selection criteria are communicated to the relevant target groups via email, during different
information events and published on the institution's website. Staff and students receive information and continuous
support during the entire application and selection process.

Regarding the target of promoting the program with the different target groups, the Hochschule Hannover has
introduced its new department for Advisory Services. Here all relevant counselling services for staff and students are
accessible in one central unit. There is individual counselling available for students with special needs as well as for
students from a disadvantaged background. Due to its commitment to diversity and inclusion the Hochschule Hannover
pays special attention to these groups. Hochschule Hannover reinforces digitalization and the development of blended
learning formats. The institution strongly supports measures in this context and therefore introduced a renewed internal
organizational structure recently in order to underline its commitment to digitalization. Concerning the promotion and
support of the realization of student mobilities the Hochschule Hannover developed and introduced its own digital
platform “Wanderlust” already in 2016. Via this platform students can network and interact with their fellow students at
home and from abroad in order to inform, prepare and support each other before, during and after their individual
mobilities. At this moment, the platform is being re-designed and further extensions as well as the introduction of new
tools are planned to continuously improve the user experience as well as to extend the digital dimension in student
mobilities.

Currently, Hochschule Hannover has broadened its perspective regarding international cooperation on a wider scale
and hosts different projects within the network of institutions within but also outside of Europe. The focus here is the
development of digital skills as well as to enhance their skills in engineering sciences through the involvement in
international applied projects. Furthermore, the same focus is taken in projects of application in cooperation with the
industrial sector.
