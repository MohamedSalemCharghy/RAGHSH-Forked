Incoming-Interview



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Steintor. I have so many memories there, because I
learned German language from A1 to B2 there. In that
duration, I have not only learnt a new language, but also I
made a lot of friends, who were learning with me together
step by step; who were spending time with me after
class; who accompanied with me in daily life, let me not
alone; who gave me the best memory in Germany.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Besides doing German language and professional
courses homework in library, I like to play football with
other countries’ students, and I like to watch the
Bundesliga. I became a member of Hannover 96. I also
like to meet my friends at the weekend, sometimes we
went to for a cup of coffee, sometimes we celebrated
someone’s birthday.


**After your semester at HannoverUniversity, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
I would like to recommend it to other students. For me, I
really had an unforgettable and wonderful experience in
Germany. It was so different with my previous life, so of
course, I’ll recommend it. If someone wants to explore
new world, Germany would be a good choice. As far, I
think the Hannover is one of my best memories in my life.


**What were you surprised about, in regard to**
**stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Patience. I found the Germans are always very patient to
deal with their work. For example: once I went to hospital.
I didn’t know exactly how to see a doctor, I asked hospital
staff, and she answered all my questions patiently. If
sometimes I couldn’t understand her, she would write the
words down on the paper, and mark it on the hospital’s
map. Moreover, the other patients had politely reminded
me what I should prepare while I was waiting outside the
consulting room.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Of course. I had decided that I would come back to
Germany for my Master studying while I was in
Hannover. So I am trying my best to achieve this thinking.

Incoming-Interview


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
1) Experience of doing Graduation project;
2) Experience of learning German language;
3) Memories of friends.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
Half crowded half quiet. Because when I was around Maschsee, I felt this city is so quiet and calm;
however, when I was watching football match, I felt this city is so enthusiastic.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes, I have lots of friends whom I made in Hannover and we still in touch with now. And they are waiting
for me coming back to Hannover.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Definitely. Because I am missing the place where has my best experience.


**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**
I was astonished that one student can stay another university’s library or canteen. But it was true, I like
Leibniz Uni’s library because it will open until 23:59 everyday. The tables, network, atmosphere and so
on, are all good enough to make me concentrate on my study.
