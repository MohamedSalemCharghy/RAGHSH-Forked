Incoming-Interview



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
I really liked the lake (Maschsee). I thought it was a really
nice place to have a coffee and relax.

**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I mostly explored the city and met friends for coffee or
drinks.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
I would recommend studying at HSH, because I enjoyed
how classes were thought in terms of student
interactions. This way of teaching has helped me get out
of my comfort zone.

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
This semester I am really enjoying sustainable
development from a management perspective. I find the
topics in this module really interesting, and the
discussions in the class make you think a lot about the
impact we are having on our surroundings.

**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
I was surprised that not all Germans are on time. I also
was not expecting German people to be as friendly as
they are, so that was a very pleasant surprise.

**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**

I really enjoyed the teaching style. I also thought the
professors were very attentive and helpful.

**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Presentations skills

- Public speaking

- A new culture experience

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
How beautiful the city was, how amazing the
transportation system was, and the friendly people.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
I would like to think so, but nothing is certain.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes I think so. I really did enjoy living in Hannover. I
especially liked the Christmas markets.
