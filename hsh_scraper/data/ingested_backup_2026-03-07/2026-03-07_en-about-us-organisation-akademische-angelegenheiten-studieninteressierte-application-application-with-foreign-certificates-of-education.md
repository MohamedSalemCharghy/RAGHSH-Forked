## Application Information for Prospective Students
If you received your educational qualifications in a foreign country, these **must** **be examined** for admission to higher education, regardless of your nationality.
This formal examination has been carried out by “uni-assist”, an institution that is operated jointly by the universities and is the work and service center for international study applications, since 1 May 2004.
The Hannover University of Sciences and Arts(HsH – Hochschule Hannover) accepts [online-only applications](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/tools/glossary-of-terms/description/details/online-only-application/) via uni assist using non-certified copies of certificates.  
An online application with uploaded documents is sufficient.
[More information on the processing costs](https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/)

## Application deadlines and special differences
[ Application deadlines and special differences ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/about-us/organisation/akademische-angelegenheiten/studieninteressierte/application/application-with-foreign-certificates-of-education "Application deadlines and special differences")
Please be sure to observe the application deadlines.
**15 November** for the summer semester and **15 May** for the winter semester  
For all admission restricted courses and Master courses
**31 May** only for winter semester  
For all artistic courses
**15 July** for winter semester and **15 January** for summer semester  
For all free admisson courses
So that it can be guaranteed that your application will be forwarded to Hochschule Hannover University of Applied Sciences and Arts in due time.
**Exceptions to these deadlines:**
For all Master’s programs in Faculty III:
  * Communication Management (MKO)


  * Television Journalism (MTV)


  * Design and Media (MDM)


the deadline for applications is**March 15.**
Only those prospective students who have submitted their complete application documents to uni-assist by **March 15** and have had those documents evaluated and approved can be considered for the selection interviews for the above-mentioned study programs.
**In addition, the following study programs also have special deadlines:**
  * Bachelor Integrated Media & Communication (IMC)
    * Apply in two steps
    * deadline uni-assist **15 May**
    * deadline faculty III **15 May**
  * Bachelor Nursing (dual)
    * Application for 1st section of study nursing until **15 October**.
    * Application for 2nd section of study nursing until **15 December** (Already in summer semester an application is necessary, as the placement test takes place at the beginning of April each year
  * Master „Entrepreneurship and Management Master Medium-Sized Company Enterprises (MMU) – apply until **15 July**


[ Special differences for some Bachelor’s degree programs ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/en/about-us/organisation/akademische-angelegenheiten/studieninteressierte/application/application-with-foreign-certificates-of-education "Special differences for some Bachelor’s degree programs")
**Special differences for the following Bachelor’s degree programs, which require a test of the student’s special artistic aptitude:**
  * Interior Design (BIA)
  * Product Design (BPD)
  * Fashion Design (BMO)
  * Scenography – Costume - Experimental Design (SKE)
  * Visual Journalism and Documentary Photography (BFO) bilingual
  * Visual Communication (BVK)
  * Media Design (BME) 


Please obtain information in advance on the 2-stage application process (1. examination of university entrance via uni-assist, 2. artistic aptitude test).
