Incoming-Interview


**Have you made friends here that you**
**will stay in touch with when you go**
**home?**
Lots of them, I got friends from Germany
and from many other counties, as Italy,
the Netherlands, Spain, Ireland and
Hungary for example. I will definitely
stay in touch with them.

**Do you think that you will come back**
**to Hannover in the future? – If so,**
**why / If not, why not?**
If I don’t move back to Hannover, I will
definitely come and visit the city to see
my friends and go to the places I have
memories from.



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
The Maschsee area, I love the views over there, and it is
really good place to go for a walk. Also, all the cafeterias
in Hannover!


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
I would definitely recommend the introduction week. I got
many really good friends also from other faculties during
that week.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I spend a lot time with my friends, went out and tried
many different cafes and restaurants.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
On the other hand, it was the best time of my life and I
really loved the city. However, in some points I was a bit
disappointed for how Hochschule worked some things
out.


**What was your favourite subject and why?**
My favorite courses were Presentation Techniques,
because the teacher was the best and also the
Sustainable Development from a Management
Perspective, the topic was really interesting and Mr.
Henning absolute great lecturer.

**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**
I liked the professors, all of them were really nice to
incoming students. Also, most of the courses were really
interesting.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Yes, it made me question if I want to live in Finland in the
future or in some other country because there’s so many
good things in Finland but at the same time I want to see
the world and get new experiences when living abroad.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**
Friends, memories and new vegan-life.

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
I love Hannover to the moon and back. The location of
the city is really good and with our semester ticket we got
to travel and see other beautiful cities as well.
