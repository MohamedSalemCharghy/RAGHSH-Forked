Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
Near Wallensteinstrasse.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Yes, because it is interesting and new experience to us.

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
Every subject, because it is different than my original
class.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**
The mensa and library.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Yes. It inspired me to travel and explore more.

**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Be independent

- Be brave

- Be friendly

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
First experience on experiencing the winter.

**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
Yes, I am still in contact with them.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Maybe. Depends on my future plan.
