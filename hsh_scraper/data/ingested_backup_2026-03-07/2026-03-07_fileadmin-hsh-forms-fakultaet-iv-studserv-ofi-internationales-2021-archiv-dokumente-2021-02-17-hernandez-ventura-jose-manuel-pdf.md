Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
At Am Papehof student hall.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and why?**
Can’t choose one, every lake or green area was a great
place to enjoy.


**What did you do during Induction Week and would you**
**recommend it?**
I joined many of the activities of the international team. I
totally recommend it, to know the city and other incoming
students.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I competed in the campus football league with other
international students, and took advantage of the student
card to travel to many cities and villages nearby.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would you**
**recommend it to other** **students and if so, why / if not,**
**why not?**
I recommend it. It was such a great experience in many
different ways. I met a lot of great fellow students, enhanced
my skills and spent a wonderful time in a beautiful city.

**What was your favourite subject and why?**
It was International Corporate Finance, because it is related
to the area I would like to develop for my professional career
in the future.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
I was surprised about their organisation at work, even
though I knew it beforehand.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**
I liked the different approach they have regarding some
subjects, which made see thing from another perspective.

**Which three key things will you take away with you from**
**your studies in Hannover?**
The people I met, the skills I developed there, and the many
places I visited.
