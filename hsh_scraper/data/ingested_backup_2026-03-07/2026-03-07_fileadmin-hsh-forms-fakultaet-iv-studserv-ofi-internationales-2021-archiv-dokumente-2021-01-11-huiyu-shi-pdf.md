Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
I stayed in an apartment, I rented it with my friends, it is
near the Ihmeplatz.

**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Central station. Because I always met friends there and it
was the place where journeys started.

**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Met friends, did some shopping and went to some scenic
spot around Hannover. I went to different countries during
holiday, like Italy, France, Norway and so on.

**What were you surprised about, in regard to stereo-**
**types, characteristics of Germans?**
I thought people are serious in German. But in fact, most
of classmates and lecturers are easy going, enthusiastic,
and some of them are humorous and funny😊.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
Katharina helped me to know way around the university,
as she is our “buddy” (she works for international office of
HsH). In daily life, yes, she helped me and my friends in
daily life as well, like help us to apply for a bank card. My
other friends and landlord helped us too. Victoria told us
there will be Halloween party and new year party and
helped us to get the tickets, parties helped us to learn more
about Germany by talking to people.

**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**

- Open mind

- Active

- Communication is important



**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover? For example; the professors, the**
**courses, the equipment etc..**
I like small meeting rooms in the building. Students can do teamwork in a relatively private space, and
there is a white board and outlet, very convenient.

**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would you recommend it to other students and if**
**so, why / if not, why not?**
Yes, I will recommend it to other students. From the perspectives of studying, the class atmosphere is
different from Chinese university, more interaction and people like to share their opinions. I think it is great.
My major is international business, so, as a Chinese in Germany, I can be more international. From other
perspectives, people I met in HsH are all very nice. International office gave me the help when I need,
many events were for incomings. Classmates were nice.

**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, I stayed in Hannover 1 year and had wonderful memories. I will come back for travelling when I have
time and enough money😊
