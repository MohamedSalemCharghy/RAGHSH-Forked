Incoming-Interview



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
I really liked going to the Christmas market at Marktkirche
because it was full of lights, food, and people were just
having a good time.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I travelled around Germany, and Europe. Met with friends
to eat and drink. Went to exhibitions and museums.


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Yes, totally. I had a great time and made a lot of good
friends from many countries.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Most people were very friendly and kind with me. They
always tried to help me when I needed it, but it was very
hard for me to make local friends.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any**
**aspect of your future plans?**
Yes, I'm no longer afraid of traveling and living in a
different country, and I would like to return to Germany in
the future either for work or to continue my studies.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**
New friends, new places, and new tools for my
professional and personal life.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
It is a great city with great people and I feel like I will
always be welcome.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
Yes, I made a lot of good friends from all around the
world.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, there are many places that I missed, and I have to
visit them someday.
