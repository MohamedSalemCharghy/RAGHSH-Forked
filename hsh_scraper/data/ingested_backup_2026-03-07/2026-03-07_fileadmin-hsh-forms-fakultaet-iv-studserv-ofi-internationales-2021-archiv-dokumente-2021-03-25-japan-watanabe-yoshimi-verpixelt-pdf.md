Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
Student Dormitory.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
The Maschsee. because it was near my dorm, and my
friends and I went there many times.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I hung out with my friends and did Tandem with German
friends.


**What did you like about studying at the university in**
**Hannover?**
BBA. IBS. Professor Austmann, Professor Peuser.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**
Friends
Participatory class
Presentation


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
Nature.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
Yes, some friends went to Japan as exchange students.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, I want to see my friends again, and also, I like
Hannover.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Be punctual.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
The International Office helped me a lot. In my daily life
usually, my German friends helped me.
