Incoming-Interview



**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Park at Leibniz as I loved to relax in the sun.


**What was your favourite subject and why?**
Economics. Always has been.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way**
**around the university, and did the same person**
**help you also in daily life, or someone different?**
Katerina. She was amazing in college and outside.


**What did you like about studying at the university**
**in Hannover? For example; the professors, the**
**courses, the equipment etc…**
The freedom and mature atmosphere compared to
Irish colleges.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**
ICM
A degree
Some friends


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in**
**touch with when you go home?**
I have. I lived with 3 of them the following year in Ireland.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in**
**the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes to the city.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Beers with friends in the Dublin Inn or relaxed with other
half.
