##  InterCultural Management Training (ICM) is designed for today’s business challenges:
###  **Join our ICM project and participate in something special!**
* * *
Working with people of different nationalities and cultural backgrounds - being efficient under extreme time and cost pressure.
![](https://f4.hs-hannover.de/fileadmin/_processed_/8/8/csm_icm2_e245e9760f.png?rbiFocus=mobile)
ICM helps you to understand different dimensions of cultures and to cope with new challenges in a positive and creative work environment.  
Become part of an international team, attend practical lectures and have fun in the Harz mountains.
![](https://f4.hs-hannover.de/fileadmin/HsH/Forms/Fakultaet_IV/StudServ/OFI/Internationales/Archiv_Bilder/2021-05-12_ICM.png?rbiFocus=mobile)
Enrollment for BBA students and incoming students during the project information session of each winter semester.
![](https://f4.hs-hannover.de/fileadmin/_processed_/1/e/csm_icm3_b51e84c4df.png?rbiFocus=mobile)
* * *
Please find below our new concept for one of the most popular international courses of our Department of Business Administration, **InterCultural Management Training (ICM)** , which is offered as a one-week-block seminar in the Harz mountains each winter semester.
ICM conveys theoretical and practical competencies in intercultural behaviour and management.
