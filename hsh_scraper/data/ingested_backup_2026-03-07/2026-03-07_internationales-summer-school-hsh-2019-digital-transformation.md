## Summer School: 10-August to 24-August-2019
### – Application Deadline: 31-March-2019
The Department of Business Information Systems of Hochschule Hannover – University of Applied Sciences and Arts will be hosting a Summer School on**Digital Transformation – Challenges and Impacts on Economy and Society.** Hochschule Hannover grants 6 ECTS points for the program. There are 1000 views on digitalization, but we all share 1 vision – a better world. Let us work together to build a better world. Therefore, share your view with us by joining our Summer School in August. Please follow us also on Instagram: @1001SummerSchool (starting in April 2019).
**Basic Idea**  
Mainly we take a 360° view of different aspects of digitalization. By doing so, we learn about the necessity to change business models, products, processes, and IT architectures. As well, we learn about the meaning of Business Intelligence and Data Mining for realizing digitalization. In intercultural workshops, the participants work in groups on the topics “challenges and impacts on economy and society”.
**Components**  
The two-week-program is composed of lectures with practical case study exercises, group work, panel discussions, an intercultural workshop, and company visits. In addition, there will be a large offer of leisure activities such as a sightseeing trip to the capital Berlin, which includes a visit of the Reichstag and the Wall Museum. There will also be a chance to get to know Hannover and its surroundings and much more.
**Target Groups**  
Undergraduate students studying Business Information Systems, Business Administration, Management Systems, Computer Science or related areas are very welcome to join the summer school. Applicants should have passed the first phase of studies.
**Participation Fee**  
The participation fee is € 250 (ca. $ 285). This will cover the participants’ accommodation, meals during lectures and official events, and the transport costs within the cities of Hanover and Berlin during the stay at summer school. Participants must prepay their travel costs to and from Hanover in full on their own, e.g. flight or train costs. However, depending on the country of origin, a partial refund of these travel costs might be granted on the basis of the Refund Policy.
