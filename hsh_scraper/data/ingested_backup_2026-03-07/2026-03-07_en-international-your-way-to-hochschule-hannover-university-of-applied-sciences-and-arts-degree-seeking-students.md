## Application - and now? How do I prepare for my studies in Germany?
Have you applied to Hannover University of Applied Sciences and Arts and perhaps already received admission? Here you will find information on how to prepare your stay and which steps you should already take in your home country.
The website "[Studieren in Deutschland](https://www.study-in-germany.de/de/)" provides comprehensive information on this topic.
The German Association of International Students ([DEGIS](https://www.degis.info/)) also provides detailed information for planning your study stay in Germany.
The Federal Government's portal provides helpful tips for students and professionals via the "[Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/de/)" website.
For your first steps and orientation in **Hannover** , the Studentenwerk has compiled everything you need to know about starting out in Hannover in the [International](https://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/international/) section.
