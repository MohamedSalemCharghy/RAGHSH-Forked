Fakultät IV, Abt. BWL; Prof. Dr. Patricia Adam
14.06.2022


**Double-Degree HsH and RGU: Admission Process – Notes for Applicants**


**Application Phase**


 You apply at the RGU for the winter term and state that you apply for the double
degree with HsH Hannover
 You send an email to patricia.adam@hs-hannover.de to inform us about your double
degree application at the RGU

 If all the requirements are fulfilled you will receive a conditional offer of the RGU

based on your enrolment in our MBD and on the successful completion of 30
credits ECTS in the pre-defined modules
The conditional offer should be open until the middle of November
 You apply for our MBD programme

 In case of a successful application you receive an admission to our MBD

programme and start attending the pre-defined modules in September


**Winter Term (MBD study at HsH, first semester of double-degree programme)**


 You get in contact with our Team International Programmes (TIP) via F4-TIP@hs
hannover.de in order to prepare your stay abroad (esp. preparation Learning
Agreement)
 You inform all your lecturers about the need for finishing your modules until 10 [th] of

January and ask them to provide you by then with a confirmation of having passed the
module (please note that the detailed grading information might be issued later)
 By the 20th of October you inform us about your willingness to pursue the double
degree by sending an email to patricia.adam@hs-hannover.de

 We will send the RGU a confirmation that you are so far “on track” with your

studies in the mid-point with our programme
 You officially enrol with the RGU until the middle of November

 The RGU provides you with all necessary documents concerning the UKVI

immigration regulations as part of the CAS process
 You prepare your time at the RGU (obtain visa, secure housing etc.)
 You finish the pre-defined modules successfully until the middle of January

 The respective lecturers send an email with the confirmation that you passed

their module successfully to our examination office (Gabi Stemmer)
 The examination office issues an official transcript stating that you fulfilled 30

credits in the required modules (usually without detailed grading)
 You hand in the transcripts at the RGU (and later the final version incl. grading)


**Summer Term (MIB study at RGU, second semester of double-degree programme)**


 You start your second part of your studies with the RGU in Aberdeen around the 20th

of January
 You successfully finish your 60 credits (including Capstone) at the RGU and receive a

Transcript of Records (eTranscript)


**Winter Term (MBD study at HsH, third semester of double-degree programme)**


 You fill out the form „Antrag auf Anerkennung der Leistungen“ and attach the Transcript

of Records (eTranscript) und the cover sheet (Deckblatt) of your Master Thesis. All is
handed in to our TIP for preparing the official acceptance for our MBD programme.
