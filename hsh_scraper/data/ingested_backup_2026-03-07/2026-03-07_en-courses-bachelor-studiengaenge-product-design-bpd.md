#  Product Design (BPD) 
##  Study product design in Hanover 
Product designers design the things in our everyday lives: Product design mediates between people and things. Studying design means taking an interest in a wide range of things and preparing for a variety of career options. This requires specialists with sound knowledge in many areas. Cultural curiosity, spatial imagination and interconnected thinking skills are just as important as confidence in dealing with materials, colour and signs.

#  The course 
[ What’s it all about? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "What’s it all about?")
This programme involves artistically applied studies that cover general creative foundations in the first two semesters. From the third semester, students specialise in one of three major subjects: Scenography, Costume or Experimental Design. An interdisciplinary exchange among these major subjects takes place in the Freestyle project in the seventh semester. The Scenography major educates students to work in theatres and associated theatre-related areas, where both stage design and narrative space for staged performances are developed.
The Costume major educates aspiring costumers for all sectors of theatre based on literature, music and dance, as well as for film and television. The Experimental Design major educates independent designers for various artistic fields of work, for instance experimental design, design processes in social contexts and design in the public sphere.
[ What will I learn? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "What will I learn?")
The programme is divided into two study phases. In the first phase (the first year), a basic artistic education (painting, drawing, performance, film, graphic print, typography) takes centre stage. Practical training is tested in art projects. In the second study period (the second to the fourth years), students concentrate on their selected majors. Corresponding to the chosen content, students carry out practically oriented semester projects that are complemented by consolidating theoretical and practical courses.
[ How will I learn? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "How will I learn?")
Our programme is based on a great degree of hands-on orientation. Practical exercises complement lectures and seminars. Project work, excursions and lectures by external teachers from the PR industry ensure a connection to current developments in the field. A further important component is the one-semester internship phase or semester abroad.
[ Who will support me? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "Who will support me?")
Apart from conveying specialised knowledge, our teachers lay great value on fostering and developing students’ social, cultural and personal skills. Our teaching staff encourage and support all students. Guest lectures and workshops led by experts from theory and practice will complement our range of courses. Hanover University of Applied Sciences and Arts has functional seminar rooms, workshops, computer labs, a well-equipped library as well as teaching and working rooms, all of which are available to students.
[ Can I study abroad? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "Can I study abroad?")
Hanover University of Applied Sciences and Arts maintains many partnerships with postsecondary institutions outside Germany. Semesters abroad are expressly encouraged and supported with scholarships.
[ How can I get work experience? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "How can I get work experience?")
The programme is based on hands-on learning. Each semester offers practical projects that are anchored in the respective professional areas of concentration. In addition, students must undergo an internship semester during their studies.
[ Where can I work later on? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "Where can I work later on?")
Depending on the major subject, graduates can work in the following areas. In Scenography, they can work in theatre, opera or associated cultural institutions. In Costume, they can start careers in national and international theatres and opera houses or film and TV production. Experimental Design offers opportunities in design, arts and culture, design projects in social contexts, museum education, adult education and other related areas.
[ Duration of studies ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/bachelor-studiengaenge/product-design-bpd "Duration of studies")
The Scenography – Costume – Experimental Design programme involves full-time study over eight semesters of regular study time. This includes the internship phase/semester abroad and completion of the bachelor project.

Guidance sessions are announced on our website at f3.hs-hannover.de/ske and take place at the Design Center. We strongly recommend participating in one of these sessions.
Apart from this, the Service Centre at Hanover University of Applied Sciences and Arts keeps all students and prospective students informed concerning study offers and study-related content. It can clarify any questions surrounding the programme, such as admission requirements, funding possibilities and changing universities or major subjects. For more information about student guidance, go to:
www.hs-hannover.de/zlb-s.

The entrance requirement for the course is demonstrated by the general university entrance qualification, the subject-related university entrance qualification, the entrance qualification for universities of applied sciences, the matriculation examination or a previous education recognised by the Ministry of Education and Cultural Affairs as being of equal value. This course always starts in the winter semester. Proof of a special artistic aptitude is required for all degree courses of the Department of Design and Media. For this purpose, either a portfolio with work samples or a motivation sketch must be submitted directly to the Department of Design and Media by 15 March together with the application for admission to the artistic entrance examination. You can find more information at [www.produktdesign-studium.de/studieninteressierte/bewerbung](http://www.produktdesign-studium.de/studieninteressierte/bewerbung).
After reviewing the samples, the university will inform all applicants whether they will be invited to the practical examination. The practical examination takes place in May and lasts one day. Good to know: You can take the entrance examination up to two years before you start your course. Further information and the application for admission to the entrance examination can be found at: [f3.hs-hannover.de/studium/aufnahmeverfahren](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/studium/aufnahmeverfahren).
