## **WELCOME TO VIENNA**

**Dear International Guest,**


The University of Applied Sciences Technikum Wien (UAS Technikum Wien) has a long tradition of
inviting members of staff and we are looking forward to welcoming you, too!

At the UAS Technikum Wien, we believe that not only you as international guest but also our
colleagues and students in-house will benefit greatly from meeting fellow lecturers as well as
administrative staff from abroad. Staff mobilities offer the unique chance to combine professional
and personal development with the idea of life-long-learning by building and growing international
networks, reflecting on internalised workflows and processes, and enhancing social and
communication skills. Simultaneously, a stay at another Higher Education Institution offers the
possibility to extend didactic skills, spread research and learning contents, and to create synergies
in education, vocational training, employability and entrepreneurship.

The guidebook at hand intends to give you an overview on our Higher Education Institution as well
as on Austria. We hope this helps you to make the best of your stay in every way and enjoy your
time at the UAS Technikum Wien!


**Sylvia Geyer**
Rector UAS Technikum Wien

## **ABOUT THE UAS** **TECHNIKUM WIEN**

####  AUSTRIA´S TECHNICAL UNIVERSITY OF APPLIED SCIENCES

With around 13,000 graduates thus far and 4,400 students, the UAS Technikum Wien is Austria’s only
purely technical university of applied sciences. The educational offer consists of 12 bachelor’s and 18
master’s degree programs, which are offered either as full-time, part-time and/or distance study
programs. Four degree programs are taught in English. The UAS Technikum Wien offers a wide array
of exciting options for gaining in-depth knowledge and specializations in a broad range of technical
fields. In addition to imparting the necessary theoretical knowledge, particular emphasis is placed on
practical applications. At the UAS Technikum Wien, emphasis is not only placed on providing a highquality technical education, but also on subjects with a focus on business and personal development.
Close ties and collaborations with business and industry give students and graduates excellent career
opportunities. The combination of theory and practical application is of central importance in both
research and instruction. The research and development activities at UAS Technikum Wien have
grown significantly in recent years and currently concentrate on the following research focuses:


          - Embedded Systems & Cyber-Physical Systems

          - Renewable Urban Energy Systems

          - Secure Services, eHealth & Mobility

          - Tissue Engineering & Molecular Life Science Technologies

          - Automation & Robotics

####  UAS TECHNIKUM WIEN ASSOCIATION


The operator of UAS Technikum Wien is the University of Applied Sciences Technikum Wien
Association (Managing Committee, management). The association bears overall financial and legal
responsibility for the university of applied sciences and is also responsible for the strategic
development and the operational management of the institution as well as for raising the public’s
awareness of the institution

## **CHECKLIST FOR YOUR** **VISIT**

####  BEFORE THE MOBILITY

If you are interested in a stay at UAS Technikum Wien please contact our staff mobility coordinator at
least 1 - 2 months prior to your visit or application deadline with the following information via Mobility
Online ( **[STT](https://www.service4mobility.com/mobility/BewerbungServlet?identifier=WIEN20&kz_bew_pers=L&kz_bew_art=IN&aust_prog=STT&sprache=en)** / **[STA](https://www.service4mobility.com/mobility/BewerbungServlet?identifier=WIEN20&kz_bew_pers=L&kz_bew_art=IN&aust_prog=STA&sprache=en)** ):


**•** Full name

**•** Your institution

**•** Your department

**•** Period of your visit

**•** Plans for your visit (teaching, research, job shadowing, etc.)



Please note that there may also be deadlines for your application at your home university.

After the International Office has received all the necessary information, the integrability of a stay is
checked and, in the best case, an allocation to a suitable counterpart takes place. The International
Office then issues an invitation and signs, for example, the Erasmus+ Mobility Agreement.

Please note that you are responsible for organizing your arrival (visa, etc.) and accommodation yourself.
Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Shortly before your stay, you will also receive a detailed program and a schedule for your stay at the
UAS Technikum.

## **ARRIVAL IN VIENNA**

####  BY TRAIN

Vienna is a European capital that can be easily reached by train. The Austrian Federal Railways
ÖBB (www.oebb.at) offer many (night) connections from neighbouring countries.


**Train Stations**

   - Wien Hauptbahnhof
more information at: **[www.hauptbahnhofcity.wien](http://www.hauptbahnhofcity.wien/)**

   - Wien Westbahnhof
more information at: **[www.westbahnhof.at](http://www.westbahnhof.at/)**

   - Bahnhof Wien Meidling

   - Bahnhof Wien Mitte

####  AT THE AIRPORT


Vienna International Airport is situated approx. 15 kilometres outside of the city. More
Information: **[www.viennaairport.com](http://www.viennaairport.com/)**

####  TO VIENNA CITY CENTRE BY TRAIN


The cheapest way to get from the airport to the city center is to use the S-Bahn S7 to the station “Wien
Mitte”. The fare is around € 4.20 (one way, this also includes public transport in Vienna, e.g. subway or
bus, if you have to change). Trains run every 30 minutes and the travelling time is around 25 minutes.

You can also use the CAT train (City Airport Train) to travel to Vienna from the airport
**[(www.cityairporttrain.com)](http://www.cityairporttrain.com)./)** . This train also takes you to the station “Wien Mitte”. The fare is about €
11 (without a ticket for public transport in Vienna). Train Interval is 30 minutes; travelling time is 16
minutes.


Once you are in the city, buy a ticket for about € 2,40 (if you have not taken the S-Bahn) at the train
station „Wien Mitte“ (above the underground station „Landstraße“ for the lines U3 and U4).

## **ADDITIONAL USEFUL** **INFORMATION**

####  CURRENCY

The currency in Austria is Euro.

####  LANGUAGE


The official language in Austria is German, English is very common as a second language.

####  EMERGENCY NUMBERS


European Emergency Service: 112
Ambulance: 144
Fire-brigade: 122
Police: 133
Emergency Medical Service: 141
Pharmacies: 1450
Intoxication: 406 43 43

Night Farmacies: **[https://www.nachtapotheke.wien/](https://www.nachtapotheke.wien/)**
Doctor´s register: **[https://www.praxisplan.at/](https://www.praxisplan.at/)**

####  TIME ZONE


Austria is located in the Central European time zone (CET = GMT / UCT + 1). Daylight saving time
applies from the last Sunday in March to the last Sunday in October (CEST).

####  ELECTRICITY


The typical mains voltage for households (as well as hotels) in Vienna is 230 volts (alternating current).

####  SHOPPING AND OPENING HOURS


Shops are usually open from Monday to Friday from 9:00 am to 7:00 pm, and on Saturdays until 5:00
pm or 6:00 pm. supermarkets are usually open Monday to Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. or 8:00
p.m. and on Saturdays until 6:00 p.m. On Sundays, shopping is only available in stores at large railway
stations.
