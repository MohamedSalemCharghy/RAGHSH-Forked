Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
In a WG.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
Maschpark because it is a pretty and relaxing place.

**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
I only did the German intensive course and I would
definitely recommend that.

**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
Absolutely. If I could do my whole bachelor as an
Erasmus student in the Hochschule I would. I loved it and
I was always very welcomed, and treated very nicely. I
also like that the courses were more practical than back
in my university.

**What were you surprised about, in regard to**
**stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
They don’t sound as aggressive as it is said, and are not
as cold. Majority of the people were very nice to me, and
tried to help me when I needed.

**What did you like about studying at the university**
**in Hannover?**
I loved the courses and the teachers, everything here
was more practical, and my assignments were very
interesting, and I always got quick feedback and help if
I needed.


**Which three key things will you take away with you**
**from your studies in Hannover?**
Put even more effort into my assignments
Be more experimental
Go out of my comfort zone more often.

**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
The perfect place to study abroad.

**Do you think that you will come back to**
**Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why**
**not?**
If the opportunity comes, I would. I love the city, it
has the perfect size, and it has everything I need.
Besides it is a very pretty place and I loved my time
there.
