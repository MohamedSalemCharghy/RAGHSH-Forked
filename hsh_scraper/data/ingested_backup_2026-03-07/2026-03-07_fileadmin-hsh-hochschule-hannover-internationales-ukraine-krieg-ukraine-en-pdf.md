# Studying in Hannover

Hannover is a student city with over 40,000 students.
There are various universities and universities of applied sciences in Hannover with a comprehensive
range of degree programmes.

## FIRST STEPS:


Check the programmes offered at the university: does the university offer my desired
study programme?


Contact the contact person at the university and get information about the application
process.


Initial check of university entrance qualifications:
[www.uni-assist.de/tools/check-hochschulzugang](http://www.uni-assist.de/tools/check-hochschulzugang)


Check financing and housing options


## WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A UNIVERSITY (UNIVERSITÄT) AND A UNIVERSITY OF APPLIED SCIENCES (FACHHOCHSCHULE)?

Universities offer teaching with a strong theoretical and academic focus, while universities of applied
sciences offer academic studies with a greater focus on practical application. Bachelor's and Master's
degrees can be earned at both types of universities. Thus, the difference lies solely in the focus of
teaching.


When it comes to the names of the individual institutions, it can sometimes get confusing because
"Hochschule" is the general term for higher education institution, including both "Universitäten" as
well as "Fachhochschulen", and universities of either type might call themselves "Hochschule" in
their German name. For example, the MHH, the TiHo and the HMTMH are "Universitäten", the
Hochschule Hannover is a "Fachhochschule".


The start of the semester also differs per university. For example, semesters at "Hochschulen" often
start as early as March and September, while semesters at "Universitäten" start in April and October.

## APPLICATION PROCEDURES AT HANNOVER UNIVERSITIES


The application procedures are not identical. At some universities a direct application is possible, at
others the application procedure runs through the central office uni-assist. The application deadlines
are also different. Application deadlines sometimes end several months before the start of the
semester (e.g. in May for the winter semester). Therefore, be sure to find out about the deadlines at
your university of choice in good time so that you do not miss a deadline.


## REQUIREMENTS FOR STUDYING: BACHELOR'S DEGREE AND STATE EXAMINATION

In order to study a bachelor's or state examination programme, you must have a university entrance
qualification (Hochschulzugangsberechtigung, HZB). The HZB is usually a school graduation
certificate, sometimes in combination with semesters from previous studies, with which you would
also be allowed to study in your home country. You can check for yourself how your qualifications
are assessed in Germany without any obligation at


www.daad.de/de/studieren-und-forschen-in-deutschland/studiumplanen/zulassungsdatenbank/
or
[www.uni-assist.de/tools/check-hochschulzugang/](http://www.uni-assist.de/tools/check-hochschulzugang/)


There, school and university degrees are evaluated and the possible study opportunities are listed.
There are three possible results:


- direct general admission.: the degree is accepted for direct study in all disciplines.

- preparatory course (Studienkolleg)*: a university qualification exam (Feststellungsprüfung)/ you
must attend a course for subject-specific preparation for the degree program before you can study.

- Subject-oriented: Those who have a subject-restricted HZB are only allowed to study certain
subjects (e.g. business, technology, natural sciences). The subject area is determined, for example, by
the subject area in which one has a school degree or study experience.


*Studienkolleg: The Studienkolleg offers one-year preparatory courses in the fields of technology,
economics, natural sciences/medicine, social sciences/languages. With the successful completion of
the Studienkolleg (via a final exam, the Feststellungsprüfung), you are allowed to apply for a study
programme in the chosen field throughout Germany.

## REQUIREMENTS FOR STUDYING: MASTER'S STUDIES


To study a master's degree programme, you must have a bachelor's degree or equivalent in a similar
field.


## LANGUAGE CERTIFICATES

Bachelor's: For enrollment in any bachelor's degree programme, you must be able to speak German at
a C1 level C1; lower language levels are sufficient for application - depending on the university.
There are no entirely English-language Bachelor's degree programmes in Hannover.


Master's: Master's programmes are offered in German or English, e.g. at Leibniz University.
Language certificates at level B2 or C1 are required for this.
For enrollment in a purely German-language degree programme, the language level C1 is usually
required, which can be proven by various language certificates. At the time of application, lower
language certificates can be submitted at some universities. Therefore, it is advisable to seek advice
directly at the university of your choice. Experience shows that the language learning process from
A1 to C1 takes 1.5-2 years.

## COSTS, FINANCING, HEALTH INSURANCE


Each university charges semester fees, which at Hanover's universities amount to 380-440 € per
semester. In addition, cost of living can amount to 800-900 € per month.


[www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/studienkosten](http://www.studentenwerk-hannover.de/studienkosten)


The financing options are varied and depend, among other things, on your residence permit. Federal
Training Assistance Act student loans (known as BAföG), scholarships and jobs are possible options.


For enrollment, proof of health insurance is usually required. If you have any questions, please
contact the university of your choice.
