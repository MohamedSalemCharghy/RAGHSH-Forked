#  workshops 
Dear students,
We as workshops support you in your projects. You can expect a team of employees with a lot of professional experience from both the crafts sector and the free market economy. Working with a wide variety of materials - from wood, to fabric, cardboard and paper, plastic or even the experimental use of unusual materials - this is what awaits you in our rooms.
Discuss your projects with us in good time so that corrections can be made and unnecessary work steps can be avoided.
**Did you already know?**  
You can book workstations via StudIP. If you have any questions, just ask us.
We look forward to meeting you!  
The workshop team
### The workshops
In a short _(german)_ video – based on an example – you can get a first insight into the workshops of our faculty. There are also guided tours of the workshops at the _[try design study design](https://www.designstudieren-hsh.de/designstudium-einblick/) (german)_ events.
Would you like to use the workshops as a student? Just come by and we'll discuss the project! Contacts to the individual workshops can be found on the subpages. Please also note the opening and closing times of the respective workshop.
We look forward to seeing you!
