#  ERASMUS+ Staff Week 
## Are you passionate about communication, supporting internationalisation, developing mobility programmes, and fostering international partnerships?
Starting on 7 October 2025, **Hochschule Hannover – University of Applied Sciences and Arts** is delighted to invite staff from our international partner institutions to participate in our 6th ERASMUS+ Staff Week, dedicated to the theme:**“Communication in an Increasingly Globalised and Digitalised World.”**
This event offers a valuable opportunity to get to know Hochschule Hannover, explore our academic programmes and services, and take part in workshops designed to inspire collaboration and exchange among peers. Our ERASMUS+ Staff Week provides an interactive platform for sharing best practices and ideas on:
  * Communicating effectively across borders and cultures
  * Selecting the right tools to reach diverse target groups
  * Developing inclusive mobility programmes and support services tailored to students’ needs


If you are looking to broaden your understanding of Hochschule Hannover, engage in meaningful peer-to-peer learning, and expand your international network, this event offers the ideal environment to connect and collaborate.
Our Staff Week is a unique opportunity to strengthen cooperation, foster mutual learning, and deepen ties in an international context. We look forward to welcoming motivated colleagues from partner institutions who are enthusiastic about sharing their experiences and contributing actively to the programme.
**Target group:**
Staff from partner institutions working with students (e.g. international officers, mobility coordinators, student counsellors, teaching staff).
To ensure diversity and a range of experiences, we are pleased to accept a **maximum of two applications per institution**.
  
**Working language:**
English
  
**Location:**
Hochschule Hannover – University of Applied Sciences and Arts (Campus Linden, Campus Expo Plaza)
  
**Date:**
7–9 October 2025
**Participation fee:**
There is **no participation fee**. Participants are expected to cover travel and accommodation costs through their institution’s ERASMUS+ Staff Mobility funds. Please direct Mobility Agreements and other relevant ERASMUS+ documents to Melanie Köster.
**Preliminary programme:**
You can find an overview of our preliminary programme with planned workshops and programme events **[here](https://typo3backend-live.hs-hannover.de/fileadmin/HsH/Hochschule_Hannover/Internationales/Wege_an_die_HsH/Staff_Week_2025_Preliminary_Programme.pdf)**.

Registration is closed.
