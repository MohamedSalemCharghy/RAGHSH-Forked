#  Health Information Management (MMI) 
##  Health Information Management 
Data and information are important commodities on the medical market of the future. In order to be able to participate in this market, the Master's course in Health Information Management offers you a qualification for leadership and management functions and specialised demanding activities in Health Information Management.
**Your course contents**
In the first semester, compulsory courses will provide you with a common competence basis. Building on this, you will deepen your knowledge in the second semester through compulsory elective modules and a project study. The elective options in the Master's course allow you to choose a course of study that is tailored to your needs, whether you have technical and information-related inclination or whether you are management-oriented: Focus on clinical data and quality management or on the application of these methods in healthcare, clinical research and drug safety.
**Your practical experience**
During your course, you will learn the skills to work scientifically based on current methods, tools and standards. The practical orientation of the degree course is not only reflected in the practical exercises that complement the lectures and seminars, but also challenges you in project work, excursions and external courses. Here and in the two practical phases, you have the opportunity to exchange ideas with external teachers from information practice on current developments in the industry.
**International opportunities for you**
The Hochschule Hannover – University of Applied Sciences and Arts also offers you a variety of international partner institutions and companies that will support you during your stay abroad and also when writing your Master's thesis. As a co-organiser of the Sixth International Conference on Mycetoma in Sudan, you will have the unique opportunity to expand your horizons in the field of health care and research abroad!

[ What's it about? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "What's it about?")
Data and information play an increasingly important role in today's medicine. Data and information management procedures and quality assurance methods include the collection, management, integration and ultimately the interpretation of information to create value.
Despite the large amounts of data that are generated today in basic medical care as well as in clinical research and development, the German university landscape has so far lacked a programme that scientifically represents this area of practice in particular.
[ What will I learn? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "What will I learn?")
In the first semester, three compulsory courses provide a basic competence base.
As early as the middle of the first semester, students are selected for one of the study specialisations: Health care and health services research as well as clinical research and pharmacovigilance. The courses in the specialisation are deepened in the second semester through a project study, among other things.
[ How will I learn? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "How will I learn?")
The study programme stands for a high degree of practical orientation. Practical exercises complement lectures and seminars. Project work, excursions and lectures by external teachers from the information practice ensure the connection to current developments in the industry.
[ How will I be supported? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "How will I be supported?")
In addition to imparting subject-specific knowledge, the development and promotion of students' social, cultural and personal competence is a priority in teaching. In this regard, students are encouraged and supported by a sufficient number of teaching staff. Guest lectures and workshops by professionals from theory and practice complement the knowledge offered in the degree programme. The facilities at the Hochschule Hannover - University of Applied Sciences and Arts include functionally equipped seminar rooms, computer laboratories, a very well-equipped library as well as teaching and work rooms for students. E-learning methods support learning and working, especially in the home environment.
[ Can I study abroad? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "Can I study abroad?")
A stay abroad is not included in the curriculum. However, the university maintains diverse contacts with educational institutions and relevant companies abroad and therefore supports students who wish to spend time abroad, especially for the purpose of writing their Master's thesis.
[ How do I gain work experience? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "How do I gain work experience?")
A compulsory internship is not included in the three-semester curriculum. However, the university uses its many contacts to relevant companies and institutions to organise the project studies and Master's thesis in a way that is relevant to practice.
[ Where can I work? ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "Where can I work?")
The Master's degree programme in Medical Information Management offers future information professionals a qualification for management and leadership functions and specialised demanding activities in medical information management in hospitals and other health care institutions as well as in clinical research and drug safety institutions and companies.
[ Duration ](https://f3.hs-hannover.de/en/courses/master-studiengaenge/medical-information-management-mmi "Duration")
The Master's programme in Medical Information Management is a full-time programme with a standard period of study of three semesters. In the first two semesters, students attend courses in the form of seminars and project work. In the third semester they write their Master's thesis.
