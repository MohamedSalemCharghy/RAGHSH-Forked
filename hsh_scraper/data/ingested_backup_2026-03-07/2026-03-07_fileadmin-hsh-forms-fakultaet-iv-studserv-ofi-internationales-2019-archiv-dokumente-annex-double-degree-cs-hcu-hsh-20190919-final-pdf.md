**Annex A**


The exchange students will follow a two-semester program of study at their host


institution. The first semester will consist of regular course work at the host institution


according to the tables below. The courses offered to double degree students will be


taught in English. The second semester consists of a master’s thesis which is supervised


by a professor of the host institution. The thesis shall be written in English language.
