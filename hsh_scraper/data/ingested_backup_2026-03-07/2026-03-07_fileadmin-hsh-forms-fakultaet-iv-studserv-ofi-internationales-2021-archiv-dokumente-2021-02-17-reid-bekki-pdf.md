Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in**
**Hannover?**
I stayed in the university’s student accommodation, just
south of Linden


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and**
**why?**
I liked the Sprengel Art Museum, and the Maschsee area
I found fascinating.


**What did you do during Induction Week and would**
**you recommend it?**
We went on a tour of the city and visited the new town
hall. We visited Bergen-Belsen and the Herrenhausen
Gardens and went to the Autostadt in Wolfsburg for the
day. I would recommend all of the items on the itinerary,
but would definitely say that Bergen-Belsen and the
Autostadt taught me the most.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I travelled around the country when I could. I visited
Berlin, Hamburg, Freiburg and Bremen and visited art
galleries and museums.


**After your semester at Hannover University, would**
**you recommend it to other students and if so, why / if**
**not, why not?**
I would recommend it as the opportunity to study in a new
way was very useful. It showed me that there can be
more than one way to work.

**Which transport did you use to get to the university,**
**and to travel around the city?**
I used the U-Bahn mostly to travel, but I walked a lot to
explore and see more of the architecture.


**Where did you have lunch?**
I and some friends would go to a falafel restaurant, as
well as some smaller cafes in the center of town.


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Everyone I spoke to was very polite and happy. I found
that people generally spoke more bluntly.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around**
**the university, and did the same person help you also**
**in daily life, or someone different?**
As I lived in the student accommodation, my flat mates
helped me with finding places, and where to go in daily
life. Within the university, there were a few people who
helped with different things, again, everyone was very
friendly and helpful.

Incoming-Interview


**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**

I liked the professors’ enthusiasm, and found that the pace of the classes were a lot slower than I was
used to. I liked the variation in courses and the ability to explore different disciplines than my own.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
I have now seriously considered moving country, and would apply for jobs outside Scotland and
England. I enjoyed learning and living within the culture and lifestyle in Germany.


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
To learn to take things slower in daily life as well as university work.
To focus more on my health and happiness.
To be more confident, and not second guess my work or my abilities within my discipline.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
The people I met made my time there so much better, full of friendships and fun.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so, why / If not, why not?**
Yes, I would like to come back and see the people I made strong friendships with, and see if the city has
changed.
