#  Master’s degree courses 
  

  
Consecutive scientific studies  

  

## Scientifically sound Master’s programme
Faculty I offers two consecutive, three-semester Master’s programmes. The prerequisite for this is an above-average degree in a seven-semester bachelor’s degree course in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology or comparable courses. The details of admission to the degree courses are regulated in the [admission regulations ](https://f1.hs-hannover.de/en/service)of the degree courses**.**
The two Master’s degree programmes are directly consecutive to the three engineering degree courses of the Faculty:
  * Electrical Engineering and Information Technology,
  * [Mechatronics](https://f1.hs-hannover.de/en/course/degree-courses-and-programmes/bachelors-degree-courses/mechatronics-mec),
  * Industrial Electrical Engineering.

**SENSOR AND AUTOMATION ENGINEERING (ESA)**
Today, sensor technology and automation are decisive factors in the development of future-proof manufacturing processes - keyword "Industry 4.0". Making industrial production and logistics processes smarter, more flexible, more efficient and safer: If these are the challenges that excite you, we look forward to receiving your application.
