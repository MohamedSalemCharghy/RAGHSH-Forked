#  Information in English 
The Career Center is an **advice and service department** for **students of all faculties** at the University of Applied Sciences and Arts. We provide an extensive range of **individual and group services** which support students to increase their **personal and professional development** and to launch their **career**.  
  
Various **tutorials and events** give comprehensive information on all aspects of searching and applying for a job, as well as develop students’ key qualifications in social and communicative areas. In addition, the Career Center staff offers **personalized advice regarding career planning and job application**.  
  
Building and strengthening connections with companies, associations, as well as with graduates, are further key tasks of our department.
### Links & more Information
Our **[events](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center/veranstaltungsangebot)** , **[advice sessions](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center/beratungsangebot)** , and **[employer database](https://firmen.cc.hs-hannover.de/)** maximize your chances of getting your dream job and facilitate your growth on a personal and professional level.
[ Our Career Training Programme ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center/information-in-english "Our Career Training Programme")
  * All our seminars aim at developing interdisciplinary skills for your study and your future job. After attending our workshops, you will understand better what competencies are required in a professional context and how to apply these.


  * Students of all faculties at the University of Applied Sciences and Arts (Hochschule Hannover) and graduates up to one year after graduation are welcome to register for our workshops.


  * We inform you regularly about our current workshops and events via your university mail account. Please check your e-mails on a regular basis so that you do not miss any of our offers.


  * During your study semester, please register for not more than five one-day-workshops in order to give other students a chance to participate as well.


[ Advice Sessions ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center/information-in-english "Advice Sessions")
We provide individual assistance with all your career related concerns like job search, job application, job interview, assessment center, career development and with every topic related to entering the job market.  
  
Our offer is designed for students of all faculties at the University of Applied Sciences and Arts as well as for graduates up to one year after graduation and it is completely free of charge.
Please apply for the individual advice session by filling out our **[online-form](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center/anmeldung-beratung)**.  
  
You will find more details on our [German website](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center).
[ Job Search ](https://www.hs-hannover.de/ueber-uns/organisation/servicezentrum-beratung/career-center/information-in-english "Job Search")

  * Students jobs
  * Trainee positions
  * Jobs for graduates
  * Companies in which you can write your bachelor or master thesis


  
In our [company data base](https://firmen.cc.hs-hannover.de/) you will find a huge variety of German and international companies and you can search according to industry and the company size.
