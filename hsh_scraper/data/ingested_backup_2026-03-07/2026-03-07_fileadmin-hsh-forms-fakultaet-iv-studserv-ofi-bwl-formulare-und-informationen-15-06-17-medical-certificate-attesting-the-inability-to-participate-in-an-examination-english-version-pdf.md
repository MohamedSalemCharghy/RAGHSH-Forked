Notes for students:

  - According to section 9, paragraph 3 of the general part of the examination regulations for
bachelor and master programs at Hochschule Hannover, the examination board has to be
provided a credible reason for the withdrawal and substantiated evidence of this reason in
written form without undue delay; otherwise, the examination will be graded as “failed”. Without
undue delay means: The submission of the medical certificate to the examination
administration [*)] has to be made within the first three days after the examination (decisive is the
receipt of the certificate), as long as the student is not prevented from submitting the medical
certificate (for example due to a hospital stay).


  - Regular sick-leave certifications (“Gelber Schein” = yellow notes) are not accepted.
