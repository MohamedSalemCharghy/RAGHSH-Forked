### Hochschule Hannover
##### University of Applied Sciences and Arts



With about 9.000 students and about 70
degree programmes, Hochschule Hannover - University of Applied Sciences and
Arts Hanover (HsH) ranks as the second
largest university in the capital of the federal state of Lower Saxony.


Founded in 1971 after the merger of several educational institutions, HsH today
is one of Hanover’s most popular universities covering an extraordinary range
of disciplines in five faculties at several
locations in Hanover.


All in all, HsH offers a wide range of applied teaching and research with a focus
on teaching and practical orientation.
Due to its industrial approach HsH has
strong ties to companies in Lower Saxony.



HsH comprises the following five
faculties:


Faculty I – Electrical Engineering and
Information Technology


Faculty II – Mechanical Engineering
and Bioprocess Engineering


Faculty III – Media, Information and
Design


Faculty IV – Business and Computer
Science


Faculty V – Diaconic Studies, Health
Care and Social Work

# The Engineering Faculties



Faculty I offers a high quality engineering education leading to a bachelor or
a master degree.


Generally, a bachelor programme takes
7 semesters to complete, including an
industrial placement. The degree programmess taught in German are accredited and internationally recognised.
Study options in the bachelor degree
programmes include standard and dual-study course programs.


With its 14 specialist subject fields, the
faculty represents important key technologies across the entire spectrum of
electrical engineering, starting with the
topics of energy supply and renewable
energies, through electromobility and
drive technology, high and very high
frequency technology, sensor technology, battery technologies and lighting
and medical technology, AI, to Industry
4.0: Industrial IT, communication networks and automation technology.

### Hannover City

Hannover is the capital of the federal state of Lower Saxony, Germany and it has more than half
a million inhabitants. Hannover as well as its visitors benefit from the variety of opportunities
offered by the city.


The quality of life is evident at every turn: top-ranking cultural and sporting events, an impressive
number of first-rate museums, fantastic shopping in one of Germany’s largest pedestrian zones as
well as the picturesque old town.


Premium tourist attractions include the famous Herrenhausen Royal Gardens and Hannover’s
unique Adventure Zoo, whilst legendary events such as the „Maschseefest“ (festival) and the world’s largest “Schützenfest” (amusement park) never fail to attract huge numbers of tourists and
locals. Visitors to Hannover particularly appreciate that everything is close at hand, such that all
of the different attractions can be reached within minutes.


If you need a place to relax or take a walk you can go to the Eilenriede – the biggest city forest in
Europe – or to the Lake Maschsee, where the mediterranean flair will make you feel like you are
on vacation.


The city center of Hannover has an extensive pedestrian area, a big shopping mall (named ErnstAugust-Galerie) and a beautiful Old Town quarter.

##### Sights in Hannover

Eilenriede
Hannover is a very green city. A huge recreational forest called Eilenriede is located in the
heart of the city. A popular destination for a daily
walk and playground for families with children.


Hannover Zoo
One of Germany‘s most spectacular zoos with more
than one million visitors per year.


Herrenhausen Royal Gardens
The Herrenhausen Gardens are an internationally
famous ensemble of garden arts and culture that
ranks among the most important historical gardens
in Europe. You can see fountains, baroque splendour
and the Grotto by Niki de Saint Phalle in the Great
Garden. Botanical trasures and greenhouses of blooming orchids can be found in the “Berggarten”.


Kröpcke
Nowadays, the centre of the city – is one of the most
popular meeting places in town. There, you will find
the main shopping streets of Hannover and a wide
range of big stores.


Fairs
A number of famous fairs take place such as
the world‘s leading trade fail for the manufacturin industry called „Hannover Messe“.



Marktkirche
The Market Church, the church at the market place,
was built in the 14th century. The market place used
to be the old city centre of Hanover.


Maschsee
The Maschsee is an artificial lake situated in the
city centre. It numerous joggers, cyclists and inline
skaters. Water sports, sailing and rowing is also possible.


New Town Hall
Built between 1901-1913, it features a magnificent
edifice dating from the Wilhelminian period, as well
as a striking domed tower.


Bars and Restaurants
Hannover has a huge number of bars, clubs and restaurants.


Tourism and Culture
Hannover has a lively cultural scene and offers activities for every taste: opera, cinema, theatre, fireworks, concerts and festivals. Moreover, Hannover
has numerous museums such as Sprengel Museum,
Landesmuseum.
