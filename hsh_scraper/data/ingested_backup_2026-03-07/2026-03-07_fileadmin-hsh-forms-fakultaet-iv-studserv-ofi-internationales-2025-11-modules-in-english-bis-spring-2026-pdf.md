**Courses offered in English**
Faculty IV – Business and Computer Science

Dear exchange students,


Please find attached information concerning courses that are available for our incoming students. In the
field of **Business Information Systems**, we currently have a range of courses on the 3 [rd] and 4 [th] year level
available in English, as listed below.


We would like to warmly recommend our Spring Welcome Weeks to you. They will take place on campus
Linden from 16 February until 27 February 2026. The welcome weeks offer you the chance of a great
start in an international environment. You will meet fellow students, build lasting friendships and get a
taste of university life while earning credits and gaining important competencies.
Details will be provided by the Incoming Team on time.


If you wish to take part in the German Intensive Course (16 February until 27 February 2026), registration
is required. You will receive an e-mail by Ms. Karin Sziedat, [Karin.Sziedat@hs-hannover.de](mailto:Karin.Sziedat@hs-hannover.de) with
information how to sign up.


**Please note: if you want to take part in the course “German as a foreign language A1” during the**
**semester, it is necessary either to have basic knowledge of the German language or to attend the**
**German Intensive Course beforehand.**


In addition to that, all guest students are invited to join our multiple language classes at different levels
(such as German, French, Italian, Spanish, and - of course - English).


Please note: The majority of credit points should be gained through courses of the Department of
Business Information Systems. **It is not possible to do ONLY language courses.**

Course title **Data mining**


Course content - Conceptual differences (Data Analysis, Inductive Statistics,
Big Data, Artificial Intelligence)

             - Data preparation (attribute types, transformations, missing
values)

              - Classical algorithms (Association Analysis, Classification, Regression,
Clustering and Outlier Detection)

              - Special cases (Text Mining, Image Classification, Time Series
Analysis)

              - Accuracy, overfitting, validation

              - Data mining projects (project types, project phases)

              - Legal and ethical aspects


Learning outcomes Students are familiar with the basic principles of data mining. They know

typical application situations and the basic structure of a number of classic
data mining algorithms. They are able to analyze the results of the
algorithms critically and comparatively. They have first experiences with the
use of data mining frameworks and know the project phases when using
data mining methods in the operational context.
This knowledge and experience enable students to structure data mining
projects. They are able to carry out independent data mining analyzes and
exchange views on complex issues with experts.
They are able to critically scrutinize the goals and results of such projects,
taking into account data protection and ethical issues.

Credits 6 ECTS
Degree course Business Information Systems
Semester Fall

Course title **Software Quality**


Course content Basic concepts of software quality

             - Quality factors and software metrics

              - Basics of software testing

              - Testing in the software life cycle

             - Static and dynamic testing

            - Test management

               - Test tools

             - Test-driven development

Learning outcomes The students can name and explain essential concepts of software quality
and its measurement. The students can name and explain essential
concepts of software testing. They can name and explain
the various testing levels in the context of the software life cycle. They can
name and explain essential aspects of test-driven development.

Credits 6 ECTS
Degree course Business Information Systems
Semester Fall and Spring

Course title **IT Entrepreneurship**


Course content Characteristics and basic conditions of the IT sector

            - Trends and development of the market

            - Different phases of managing an enterprise formation

            - Generating and evaluating business ideas

            - Market and competitor analysis

             - Protection of business ideas

            - Drawing up a business and finance plan

             - Content of a business plan: marketing, sales, service, organization,
legal form, financing

             - First operating steps

Learning outcomes The students will know the success and failure factors of
enterprise formation and the special aspects in the IT sector. They train the
single steps of an enterprise formation by generating and evaluating their
own business ideas and drawing on their own real business plans. Every
student can evaluate his/her own suitability to become an entrepreneur.

Credits 6 ECTS
Degree course Business Information Systems
Semester Fall and Spring


Course title **Corporate Performance Management**



Course content




- Business management concepts

- Data origin of strategic controlling instruments

- Data origin of business intelligence (BI) and dashboard

- Development of strategies based on enterprise and business area

- Business simulation game



Learning outcomes The students are able to apply and to assess key performance indicators in
terms of business management and controlling. They can make general
business decisions independently and understand the dependencies
regarding individual indicators as well as their values. They are familiar with
the fundamental methods of data sourcing from business intelligence (BI)
and controlling processes. By dealing with complex case studies, use cases
and simulations in small groups they can solve business problems
independently and holistically.

Credits 6 ECTS
Degree course Business Information Systems
Semester Fall and Spring

Course title **Database Paradigms**


Course content Alternatives to relational databases. Data modelling, query languages,
application programming, internal concepts and implementation details
for e.g. OO/OR, XML, graph, document and key/value databases.

Learning outcomes Analysis and design skills: Recognize usage scenarios for alternative DB
paradigms; ability to select the correct model / system
for a given problem. Design of applications based on alternative DB
paradigms.
Algorithmic skills: Knowledge of modern DB algorithms, especially memory
organization and query processing. Knowledge of the pros and cons of the
algorithms for a given problem.
Technological skills: Knowledge of query languages and access APIs.

Credits 6 ECTS
Degree course Applied Computer Science
Semester Fall 2025 | Usually offered every two years


Course title **Presentation Techniques**


Course content This course includes: research into an academic topic in detail¸ preparing
and delivering short oral assignments; working in small groups for in-class
tasks; learning to structure a presentation effectively; giving constructive
feedback to peers.

Learning outcomes On completion of this module the student should be able to: self-organize
and structure an oral presentation in English; present confidently in front
of a small group; arrange information effectively in speech and on
presentation slides; discuss and answer questions on their topics and
critique each other; effectively deal with anxiety before public speaking
occasions; use appropriate vocabulary, verb tenses and sentence structure
when giving a presentation.

Credits 3 ECTS
Degree course International Business Studies
Semester Fall and Spring
