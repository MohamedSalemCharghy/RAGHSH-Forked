Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
I stayed at Sarah Lange’s flat – she was studying abroad
the same semester so I rented her room.


**Which was your favourite place in Hannover and why?**
The Maschsee


**What did you do during Induction Week and would you**
**recommend it?**
We got to meet and become friends with all the other
students doing various activities around Hannover. It was
very fun and I do recommend it.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
Traveled around Europe, visited breweries, watched the
World Cup, explored Hannover


**After your semester at Hannover Hochschule, would**
**you recommend it to other students?**
Yes absolutely I would recommend it. I loved the
university, the teachers, the students, the city, and all the
friends I made there. I miss it a lot.


**What was your favourite subject and why?**
International business. I loved learning about different
countries and companies.


**Which transport did you use to get to the university,**
**and to travel around the city?**
U-Bahn and a lot of walking


**What were you surprised about, in regard**
**to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
Their sense of humor is very different from Americans. But
they are very nice and welcoming.



**What did you like about studying at the university in Hannover?**
I really liked everything about it. The professors were great and we got to go on field trips. The other
exchange students and German students were so nice.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
I really wanted to live in Germany but unfortunately I haven’t learned the language so I don’t think it
would be possible.


**Which three key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**

   - Having friends from different backgrounds is important to keeping an open mind

   - Being in a city where you don’t speak the language helps you learn how to be independent and
that is a priceless experience for a young adult

   - Germans have amazing beer

Incoming-Interview


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
It was where I spent some of the best months of my life and I will always think back on it fondly.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes I still stay in touch with many people that I met there.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future?**
Yes I would love to come back and visit. I need to have some Herrenhäuser beer and Lüttje Lage 😊😊
