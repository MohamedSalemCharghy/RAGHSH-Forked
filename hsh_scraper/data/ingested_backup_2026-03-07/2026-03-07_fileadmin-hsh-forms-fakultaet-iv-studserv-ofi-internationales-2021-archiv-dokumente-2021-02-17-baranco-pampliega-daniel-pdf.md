Incoming-Interview



**Where were you staying while studying in Hannover?**
I was living in one of the public residences of Hannover,
thanks to the Studentenwerk, at Stadionbrücke.


**Which was your favorite place in Hannover? And**
**why?**
All the Old Town, because it was one of our iconic
meeting points and we could spend a lot of time in pubs,
restaurants, and shops.


**What did you do in Induction Week and would you**
**recommend it?**
I remember great activities such as karaoke, volleyball at
Hochschule, a guided tour of the Volkswagen factory,
jumping, pub crawl… And of course, I would recommend
all of them. It is a fabulous opportunity to meet new
international people, and joining the first activities of
Erasmus.


**What did you do in your spare/free time?**
I always tried to stay with people, and organize plans
based on travels around Germany and other countries
like Poland, France and Belgium. Also, we spent a lot of
time in other residences; went for a party occasionally (I
recommend it, for sure 😊) or to the cinema. On
Mondays, we went bowling and every Thursday to karting
for example.


**After your semester at Hannover University would**
**you recommend it to other students and why?**
Totally. At the beginning of it, I thought it was a normal
destiny but afterwards, I discovered that Hannover is a
city whose universities offer great opportunities and
activities. A city with good public transport communication
(and close to Berlin, Hamburg, and Bremen); special
nightlife and a good choice to learn a new language.


**What was your favorite subject and why?**
ICM (International Cultural Management). No doubt about
it. I think all the modules had something special, but this
one was teaching in the Harz Mountains during 5-6 days,
and it was more than a ‘normal subject’, it was an
experience to know how to work side-by-side with the
rest of the people. Very, very funny.

Incoming-Interview


**What were you surprised about, in regard to stereotypes, characteristics of Germans?**
I found all kinds of people, very open-mind Germans, and others who are not. But in general, people could
indeed be less ‘warm’ than in Spain. Although that is not a bad thing either. Another point was that the
way of working seemed much more effective to me, very systematic, and everything planned. That was
curious and very German haha. One more thing was, what do people eat in Germany? All men are very
tall and strong, and some women too! Haha.


**Buddy Topic: Who helped you find your way around the university, and did the same person**
**help you also in daily life, or someone different?**
In general, I did it myself, but I have to say that Jasmin and Franzy helped us so much with our daily
troubles, and in most modules.


**What did you like about studying at Hannover university?**
The most are the teachers and the courses. Also the people, obviously, but in general I loved the
atmosphere in all the modules with the teachers, and how we created daily projects with different
groups. All this surprised me so much.


**Has studying in Hannover made you rethink any aspect of your future plans?**
Well, now I’m still studying German and it was something I never thought about, haha. After this, I feel
more inspired to live in Germany, or another country, and look for a job abroad. Indeed, I’m still doing it.


**What 3 key things will you take away with you from your studies in Hannover?**
The experience, my friends, and the feeling that I have a new home forever far away from Madrid.


**What lasting impression will you have of Hannover?**
My home. It is not New York, or Berlin, or London, or Paris, but each corner is a special place for me.


**Have you made friends here that you will stay in touch with when you go home?**
Yes, I have. We have a WhatsApp group, and we talk all the weeks and about plans in Spain and
Europe.


**Do you think that you will come back to Hannover in the future? – If so why/If not why not?**
For sure. In my opinion, Hannover is my second home, and I want to come back for a weekend or a
week. Or who knows, probably forever haha.
